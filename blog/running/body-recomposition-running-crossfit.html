Body Recomposition for Hybrid Athletes: Lose Fat, Keep Muscle With Running and CrossFit

Updated August 2026 · 4 min read

Recomp means better fat-to-lean ratio — not just a lighter scale. Over 8–16 weeks you want fat down, lean mass stable or slightly up, and performance held (easy pace at the same HR, lifts within ~5%, WOD scores not collapsing). Favorable conditions: beginners, returning athletes, or higher starting body fat. Very lean athletes usually need a surplus to build, then a cut.

Key Points

  • Deficit: ~200–400 kcal/day average (~0.2–0.4 kg fat/week when estimates are honest). Details in calorie deficit for runners
  • Protein: 1.6–2.2 g/kg (bias 1.8–2.2 in a deficit); ~110–155 g for 70 kg — see protein guide
  • Strength 2×/week: squat/hinge/push/pull, 5–8 reps, RPE 7–8, 35–50 min — not random thrusters counted as “lifting” (strength for runners)
  • Zone 2: 150–220 min/week for most hybrid athletes; set with the Zone 2 Calculator
  • Hard metcons: 2–3/week; cap total hard sessions (strength + intervals + metcons) at ~4–5/week

Plan from trend weight and training quality. Wearables often err ±10–20% on calories — use the Running Calories Calculator for run estimates, not single-session watch totals.

Sample Hybrid Week

  • MonStrength A 40 min + optional 20–30 min Zone 2
  • TueMetcon scaled to 12–15 min hard work
  • WedZone 2 run 45–60 min
  • ThuStrength B 40 min
  • FriEasy Zone 2 30–40 min or rest
  • SatOptional short metcon or long Zone 2 60–75 min
  • SunRest

Schedule mileage against box days with Running and CrossFit Training Balance.

Practical Takeaway

Mild deficit + high protein + two real strength days + repeatable Zone 2. Keep carbs around hard sessions. If RHR is up 5–8 bpm for 5+ days, easy pace slows at the same HR, or scores drop >10% for two weeks — fix recovery before cutting more food.

FAQ

Why isn’t the scale moving?

Recomp often shows first in waist, clothes, and pace-at-HR. Use a 7-day weight average; day-to-day ±0.5–1.0 kg is normal fluid/glycogen noise.

Should I cut harder to see faster results?

Deep deficits (750–1,000+ kcal) raise lean-mass loss risk when you’re also stacking runs and WODs. Prefer adding 20–40 minutes of Zone 2 before slashing another 300 kcal.
