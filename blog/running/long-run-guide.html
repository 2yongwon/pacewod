The Long Run: Benefits, Pacing, and Fueling

Updated August 2026 · 5 min read

The long run builds endurance — until you turn it into a weekly half-marathon time trial. That conflict (aerobic stimulus vs ego pace) is why Monday feels destroyed and Tuesday quality dies. Keep most long runs conversational Zone 2 with the Zone 2 Calculator and Zone 2 guide; save race-pace finishes for planned workouts inside a plan like the half marathon plan.

How Long Is Long Enough?

Time often beats distance: 75–150 minutes covers most recreational goals. Marathon peak long runs may stretch longer, but jump size matters more than a single heroic Sunday. Terrain and heat count as hidden intensity — a hilly hot 90 minutes can stress you like a cooler flat two hours.

Numeric scenario: Current long run 14 km in ~1:25 at easy effort. Build 14 → 15 → 16 → 14 (cutback) → 17 → 18 → 16 over seven weeks (~1 km most weeks, never +3 km jumps). If finishing HR drifts >10% above early-run easy HR at the same pace, slow down or add a walk break — don’t “tough out” cardiac drift from heat or underfueling. Rough energy check: Calories Calculator.

Expert Note

Fueling practice belongs in the long run if you’ll race over ~90 minutes. Start with ~30–60 g carbs/hour on longer efforts and adjust. Debuting a new gel brand on race day is a common own-goal.

Decision rule: if the last third requires grinding into Zone 3–4 just to hold “easy” pace, the run was too long/fast or you underfueled — shorten next week’s long run by 10–20% and fix fueling before extending again.

Pacing Patterns That Work

  • Even easy effort for most weekends
  • Optional last 15–20 min at marathon/half effort late in the cycle
  • Walk breaks are tools, not failure — especially in heat

Predict race fitness with the Race Predictor from a recent race, not from one ambitious long-run average pace. Social long-run groups are great until the pack’s “easy” is your tempo — know your ceiling and let them go.

Practical Takeaway

Protect the long run’s job: durable aerobic time. Progress distance slowly, stay mostly easy, and practice fueling before race day.

FAQ

Should long runs include speed every week?

No. Sprinkle controlled finish segments in peak weeks; keep most Sundays truly aerobic.
