Zone 2 vs Zone 3 Running: What Changes and When to Use Each

Updated August 2026 · 5 min read

On a flat 50-minute loop, 5:50/km at 142 bpm feels easy; the same loop at 5:25/km and 158 bpm feels “productive” — and steals recovery from tomorrow’s intervals. Zone 2 vs Zone 3 is not a branding fight. It is whether today’s run was recovery with a purpose or accidental moderate load.

Ranges That Actually Differ

  • Zone 2: ~60–70% HRR — conversational, smooth
  • Zone 3: ~70–80% HRR — “comfortably hard,” short sentences

Personalize with the Heart Rate Zones Calculator and Zone 2 Calculator. Wrong math → wrong decisions. Pair HR with talk test and pace from the Pace Calculator when wrist HR lags on hills.

Worked Scenario: Four Medium Runs

Runner A: Zone 2 ceiling 148 bpm. Four midweek “easy” runs average 5:30/km at 156–160 bpm. On paper: 40 km of base. In load terms: four Zone 3 sessions and no easy days. Hard Thursday intervals flatten. Fix: cap those runs at ≤148 bpm even if pace slows to 5:55/km — then the one planned 35-minute steady at 155–160 bpm becomes useful, not chronic.

Expert Note

Do not ban Zone 3 forever. Planned steady runs, marathon-pace blocks, and progression finishes belong there. The failure mode is unplanned Zone 3 every day because average pace looked respectable on Strava.

Choose Before You Lace Up

  • Default Zone 2 after poor sleep, when sore, or when you already have 1–2 hard sessions that week.
  • Use Zone 3 for specific stamina: 30–45 min steady, or 2–3 controlled blocks in a long run.
  • Walk hills on easy days; grinding uphill into Zone 3 does not “still count as easy.”

Progress markers: faster pace at the same Zone 2 HR over 4–8 weeks; less late-run drift. Related: Zone 2 guide.

Decision Rule

If the calendar says easy and HR sits in Zone 3 for more than a few minutes, slow until Zone 2 returns. If the calendar says steady/tempo and you planned Zone 3, hold it with a time cap — then go easy the next day.

Edge Case

Is one Zone 3 run a week a problem?

No — one intentional steady session can bridge base and threshold. Four accidental ones erase the easy days that make hard days work.
