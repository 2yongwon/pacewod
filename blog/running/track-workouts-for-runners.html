Track Workouts for Runners

Updated August 2026 · 4 min read

The track removes guesswork: fixed distances, consistent surface, honest splits. Use it for VO2 and speed-endurance work where pacing precision matters more than scenery.

Distances and Lanes

  • Standard outdoor lap (lane 1) = 400m
  • Common reps: 200 / 400 / 800 / 1200 / 1600m
  • Outer lanes add distance per lap — start races/workouts from proper stagger or use lane 1–2 for measured reps
  • Indoor tracks vary (often 200m) — adjust lap counts

Split charts: Pace Chart. Session ideas also in Interval Training and 5K Plan.

Etiquette Essentials

  • Faster traffic inside; joggers/recovery outside
  • Pass on the right (or local custom); don’t stop abruptly in lane 1
  • Look before changing lanes; headphones low enough to hear

Session Template

  1. 15–20 min easy + drills/strides
  2. Main set (examples below)
  3. 10–15 min easy cool-down
  • VO2 400s: 8–10 × 400 @ 3K–5K, 200m jog
  • Speed-endurance 400s: 12 × 400 @ 5K with short float recovery
  • VO2 800s: 5–6 × 800 @ 5K, 400m jog
  • Threshold 800s: 6 × 800 @ 10K, 60–90 s stand/jog

Pacing Tips

First rep slightly restrained. If split 1 is your fastest, you started wrong. Use a watch lap button at the line; don’t trust GPS alone on a small oval.

400m and 800m Pace Reality

At 5:00/km (8:03/mile), 400m ≈ 2:00 and 800m ≈ 4:00. At 4:00/km, 400m ≈ 1:36. Write split targets before the session. Outdoor wind and lane changes matter — don’t “make up” time with a chaotic last 50m that wrecks the next rep.

Indoor tracks: tighter turns, often shorter laps — reduce volume slightly the first session while you adapt.

FAQ

GPS or track markings?

Trust lane-1 markings and the lap button. GPS on a 400m oval often weaves and misreads splits — especially in urban stadiums.

When Track Beats Roads

Choose the track when split accuracy matters (VO2 400s/800s) or when you keep surging on roads. Prefer roads/trails for easy volume and long runs. One precise track session beats three sloppy “fast” road efforts with unknown distances.

Practical Takeaway

Pick one track session per week in a quality block, nail even splits, and leave ego for race day. The track rewards control more than hero first laps.
