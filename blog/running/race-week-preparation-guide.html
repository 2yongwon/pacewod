Race Week Preparation: Taper, Nutrition, Sleep, and What to Skip

Updated August 2026 · 4 min read

Seven days out, legs feel heavy and you want to “test” fitness, buy new shoes, or carb-binge at midnight. Race week is not a training week. Fitness is already built — the job is to arrive fresh, fueled, and boringly consistent.

Taper Volume and Short Sharpeners

Cut total weekly volume ~40–60% from peak while keeping short race-pace touches (strides or a few goal-pace reps). Fitness does not vanish in seven easy days; fatigue does. Skip threshold tests and heroic course recon. Distance notes: 5K/10K taper is shorter and sharper; half/marathon need more volume cut and earlier bedtime discipline. See Tapering Before a Race for length by distance.

Carbs, Gear, Sleep

  • Carbs: For longer races, bias carb-forward meals the final 2–3 days (~higher daily carb intake, not one giant pasta dinner). Keep fiber and brand-new foods low if your gut is sensitive.
  • Gear: Nothing new on race day — shoes, socks, gels, sports bra, watch face. Pack the night before.
  • Sleep: Prioritize the two nights before race night; poor sleep the night before is common and less damaging if sleep debt is low.

Anxiety Mistakes to Skip

No new aggressive stretching routines, no hyperhydration with plain water only, no scrolling start lists until 1 a.m., no last-minute shoe purchases. If you have not tested it in the last three weeks, skip it. Heavy legs mid-week are common — do not add miles to “shake them out.”

Race Morning Checklist

Wake with buffer time. Eat the practiced breakfast (Race Day Nutrition). Sip fluids with sodium as planned. Easy activation jog + strides if that is your routine. Start the watch after GPS lock. Trust the taper.

Practical Takeaway

Cut volume, keep a little intensity, eat familiar carbs, sleep, and pack early. Race week rewards restraint.

FAQ

Why do my legs feel heavy mid-taper?

Common as training load drops and glycogen/water shift. Do not interpret heaviness as a reason to run more. Keep easy volume low and trust short race-pace touches.

What should I avoid in race week?

Fitness tests, new gear/foods, huge one-night carb binges, hard recon runs, and anything untested in the last three weeks.
