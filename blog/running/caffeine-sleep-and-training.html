Caffeine, Sleep, and Training: Cutoff Times, Dose, and Race-Day Trade-offs

Updated August 2026 · 4 min read

Caffeine is a proven ergogenic aid — and a common quiet sleep stealer. Average half-life is ~5–6 hours, so afternoon doses often linger at bedtime. Pair with race day nutrition and sleep and running performance.

Key Points

  • Cutoff: finish caffeine 8–10 hours before target bedtime (safer than a 6-hour rule at athletic doses of 200–400 mg)
  • Race dose: often 3–6 mg/kg ~45–60 min pre-start; above ~6–9 mg/kg raises side effects without reliable extra benefit
  • Examples: 60 kg → 180–360 mg; 75 kg → 225–450 mg — practice in training, never debut a huge dose on race morning
  • Evening WODs/intervals: prefer non-stim pre-workout, carbs + warm-up, or shift quality earlier — see CrossFit recovery
  • Wearables: late caffeine often shows as higher overnight HR and worse sleep scores — pharmacology, not “weak recovery”
  • Habitual high intake: don’t cold-turkey mid-taper; gentle reduction or stable morning dose + small race top-up is safer
  • Read labels: coffee, gels, and pre-workouts stack faster than athletes expect

Training-Day Matrix

  • Morning hard session: usual dose OK; keep afternoon clear
  • Evening hard session: ≤100 mg early afternoon or none; don’t stack coffee + gel + pre-workout
  • Easy Zone 2 day: minimal or none — use the Zone 2 calculator and save stim for key work
  • A-race (morning): 3–4 mg/kg is enough for many; bank sleep on nights −3/−2
  • Evening race: use the low end (~3 mg/kg), avoid all-day stacking, accept night −0 may be imperfect

Slow caffeine metabolizers feel late doses harder. If sleep onset regularly stretches past 30 minutes after afternoon coffee, move your cutoff 60–90 minutes earlier for two weeks and reassess with RHR/HRV trends.

Practical Takeaway

Reserve aggressive dosing for key sessions and races. Protect cutoff times on routine days so sleep can absorb the training load you worked for.

FAQ

Is a 6 p.m. pre-workout fine if I sleep at midnight?

Often no at 200–300 mg — you may still have ~100+ mg on board near bedtime. Move earlier, cut dose, or go non-stim.

Should I quit caffeine all week before a race?

Evidence is mixed; withdrawal can wreck taper days. Prefer modest, timed intake over heroic abstinence.
