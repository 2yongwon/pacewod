Polarized Training (80/20) for Runners

Updated August 2026 · 5 min read

Your calendar shows two hard days and five easy days — 80/20 on paper. Your HR files show three hard days and four medium days. Polarized training only works when “easy” is truly easy. Roughly 80% of time low intensity / 20% high intensity beats living in the gray zone that feels productive but stalls progress.

Count Minutes, Not Run Labels

Easy = Zone 1–2 (conversational). Hard = threshold and above (tempo, intervals). Zone 3 steady work is limited and intentional. Set zones with the HR Zones Calculator; keep easy days honest per the Zone 2 Guide and easy-run mistakes.

Worked Week: ~50 km

Target: ~50 km. Tue: 6 × 3 min VO2 (~25 min hard). Thu: 30 min tempo (~30 min hard). Remaining ~40 km Zone 2 with a long run. Hard time ≈ 55 min; if total running time is ~280 min, hard ≈ 20%. Broken version of the same week: those “easy” days average 155 bpm (Zone 3) — hard+moderate jumps past 40% and Thursday tempo dies. Fix with the Zone 2 Calculator ceiling before adding more intervals (interval guide). Race targets: Race Predictor.

Expert Note

Do not force pure polarized every week of the year. Race-specific phases can include planned steady/marathon-pace work. Also do not count 6 × 20 s strides as the week’s hard dose unless you turned them into a sprint session.

Gray-Zone Traps

  • Every run moderately hard
  • Hard day not actually hard (tempo shuffle forever)
  • Counting runs instead of zone time

Sample Shapes by Volume

~30 km: one interval day, everything else Zone 2. ~50 km: one VO2 day + one tempo, remaining easy. ~70 km: same two quality days with longer easy volume; optional strides after an easy run improve mechanics without counting as the hard dose.

Four-Week Implementation

Week 1: fix easy-day HR caps and accept slower pace. Week 2: schedule exactly two quality sessions. Week 3: audit time-in-zone. Week 4: progress either volume or one quality variable. That sequence beats declaring “I do 80/20” while every easy run sits in Zone 3.

Decision Rule

If a week’s time in moderate + hard exceeds ~25–30% without a race-week reason, slow every non-quality run under your Zone 2 ceiling for the next two weeks before progressing volume or intensity. If easy compliance is already solid, progress either weekly volume or one quality variable — not both in the same week.
