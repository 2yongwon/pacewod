Best Heart Rate for Fat Burn

Updated August 2026 · 5 min read

Gym poster says 130 bpm for “fat burn.” Your 45-minute jog at 130 burns fewer total calories than the same 45 minutes at 150 — and fat loss still follows the week’s energy balance, not the percentage of fuel from fat in one session. The fat-burning zone is a half-truth that hijacks training decisions.

What Peak Fat Oxidation Actually Means

At easy aerobic effort (~Zone 2), a higher share of fuel comes from fat. At harder efforts, carb share rises and calories per minute usually rise too. For scale weight, weekly expenditure and intake matter more than maximizing fat % every run. Set zones with the Zone 2 Calculator and HR Zones Calculator; estimate burn with the Running Calories Calculator.

Worked Scenario: 72 kg Runner, Two Sessions

Same athlete, two options. (A) 50 min at 138 bpm (~Zone 2) ≈ ~580–620 kcal. (B) 35 min easy + 8 × 1 min hard / 1 min jog ≈ similar or higher total burn, higher fitness stimulus, more residual fatigue. For fat loss across a week, (A)×4 plus one quality session usually beats living only at the poster’s peak-fat HR with tiny weekly minutes. Appetite and “eat back” habits decide the scale more than either single workout.

Expert Note

Do not use watch “calories from fat” charts to plan meals. Optical HR errors and proprietary models distort those splits. Prefer weekly body-weight trend and workout quality over a fat-kcal tile.

Zone 2’s Real Job (Not Magic Furnace)

  • Builds aerobic base so you can handle more weekly volume
  • Improves fat oxidation capacity for endurance
  • Keeps recovery cost low so limited hard work still lands

Avoid gray-zone “easy” days (Zone 3) that raise fatigue without much fitness payoff. Cross-check pace with the Pace Calculator; VO2 context: VO2 Max Calculator.

Decision Rule

If the goal is fat loss and you can only train 3–5 hours/week, keep most runs in honest Zone 2 and add at most one hard session — then set food from a modest weekly deficit, not from maximizing “% fat burn” on every jog. If weekly volume is already high and recovery is solid, do not slow every run solely to chase a poster zone.

Edge Case

Is low HR better than intervals for fat loss?

Low HR supports more weekly minutes. Intervals help fitness and burn. Mix both; living only at either extreme usually underperforms.
