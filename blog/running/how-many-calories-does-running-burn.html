How Many Calories Does Running Burn?

Updated August 2026 · 4 min read

Two runners can cover the same distance at the same pace and still burn different calories. Treat estimates as ranges. A useful first pass is distance-based; MET math adds pace and time; watches add proprietary noise.

Distance Rule and MET Formula

Shortcut: ~1 kcal per kg body weight per km (about 0.75–1.0 kcal per pound per mile) on level, steady running. Example: 70 kg × 10 km ≈ 700 kcal.

MET: Calories/min ≈ (MET × kg × 3.5) / 200. Easy running is often ~9.8 MET; moderate ~10.5; tempo ~11.5–12+. A 70 kg runner at 9.8 MET for 45 minutes ≈ 550 kcal. Hills, wind, and surges push cost up; better economy can nudge it down. Add ~10–15% on courses with substantial climbing versus the flat shortcut.

Rough Distance Ranges

  • 5K: ~250–450 kcal
  • 10K: ~500–900 kcal
  • Half: ~1,100–2,000 kcal
  • Marathon: ~2,200–4,000+ kcal

Body mass is the biggest driver; terrain and heat matter next. Faster pace raises burn per minute more than per kilometer. Walking the same distance typically burns roughly 60–70% of running for the same person.

Why Watches Disagree

Devices mix speed, HR, and profile data; some show total calories (including resting) vs active only. Garmin and COROS often differ 15–25% on the same run. Optical HR errors move calorie models with them. Prefer weekly trends over single-run precision, and compare against the Running Calories Calculator. Details: watch calorie accuracy.

Fat Loss Context

Running helps a deficit, but appetite and “eat back” habits often erase the spreadsheet win. Use calorie ranges to plan fueling for long runs and to sanity-check weekly expenditure — not to justify matching every watch calorie with food. Pair with running for weight loss if composition is the goal.

Practical Takeaway

Use three anchors — distance rule, MET, watch — and take the middle. For fat loss, do not eat back the highest number. Keep easy days truly easy with the Zone 2 Calculator; volume consistency beats maximizing one run’s calorie readout.

FAQ

Does running faster burn more per mile?

Slightly, but not proportionally. Duration and total weekly volume usually matter more than turning every run into a sprint.

Should I eat back all watch calories?

No. Wearables often overestimate. Use them as a rough weekly input and judge by multi-week weight and performance trends.
