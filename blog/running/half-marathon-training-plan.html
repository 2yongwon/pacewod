12-Week Half Marathon Training Plan

Updated August 2026 · 4 min read

This 12-week half marathon plan is for runners who can already run ~45–60 minutes easy and want a structured build. Phases: aerobic foundation → build → sharpen/taper. Set easy intensity with the Zone 2 Calculator and easy run pace guide; check goals with the Race Predictor and Pace Chart. Calorie awareness: calories calculator. Predictors lie sometimes — read why predictors are often wrong.

Key Points

  • Weeks 1–4: easy volume + short tempo at HM effort
  • Weeks 5–8: longer tempo/long runs; optional faster reps
  • Weeks 9–12: peak specific work, then taper
  • Race day: first 5K patient; fuel/practice what you trained

Phase Schedules

Typical week (Weeks 1–4)

  • MonEasy 40 min
  • TueTempo 20–25 min at HM pace
  • WedEasy 35 min
  • ThuEasy 40 min
  • SatLong run — 70 min (W1), 75 min (W2), 80 min (W3), 65 min easy (W4 recovery)

Week 6 example

  • MonEasy 45 min
  • TueTempo 35 min at HM pace
  • WedEasy 40 min
  • Thu4 × 1 mile at 10K pace, 2 min jog
  • SatLong 90 min (last 25 min at HM pace)

Week 8 example (peak week)

  • MonEasy 45 min
  • TueTempo 40 min at HM pace
  • WedEasy 40 min
  • ThuEasy 45 min
  • SatLong 95 min

Week 10

  • MonEasy 40 min
  • Tue3 × 1 mile at HM pace
  • WedEasy 35 min
  • SatLong 75 min

Week 11

  • MonEasy 35 min
  • TueTempo 20 min at HM pace
  • WedEasy 30 min
  • SatLong 60 min

Week 12 (race week)

  • MonEasy 25 min
  • Wed3 × 1K at HM pace
  • FriRest
  • SunHalf marathon

When to Adjust

Cut intensity before volume when fatigued. If long runs repeatedly blow up, extend the foundation phase instead of forcing peak mileage. One missed week ≠ crisis; three ignored easy days is.

Practical Takeaway

Half marathon success is boring easy miles, one honest quality stimulus, and a long run that teaches patience. Taper and race the plan you actually completed.

FAQ

What starting mileage do I need?

Comfortable with ~30–45 min easy runs most days of a week is a practical floor. If not there yet, finish a 10K block first.

Should long runs include race-pace miles?

Later in the build, short HM-pace segments can help — keep most of the long run easy so recovery survives.
