Watch Calorie Accuracy: Why Garmin, COROS, and Apple Watch Overestimate Burn

Updated August 2026 · 4 min read

Watch: 812 kcal. MET: ~640. Treadmill: 590. For Garmin, COROS, and Apple Watch, the device total is often the highest — and the weakest number for precise nutrition. Wearables are great for trends; mediocre as lab instruments.

What the Watch Is Estimating

Calories are modeled from profile data, GPS/accel movement, heart rate, and proprietary engines (e.g., Firstbeat-style analytics). Confirm active vs total — Garmin totals often include basal burn during the activity window. Apple separates Active vs Resting more clearly but workout tiles still confuse people. Logging “total” as “active” invents snacks you did not earn.

MET Check and Typical Error

Calories/min = (MET × kg × 3.5) / 200. Example: 70 kg, 50 min easy (~9.8 MET) ≈ 600 kcal; distance rule ≈ 570–620. A watch at 780 is roughly +25–30%. Expect ±15–25% (sometimes more) on intervals, trails with hiking, strength/metcons, heat drift, and wrong body-weight profiles. Steady Zone 2 with a chest strap and correct weight is best-case — still treat ±10% as noise.

Worked comparison: 70 kg, 10 km in 55 min → distance ≈ 700, MET (~10.5) ≈ 707; an optical-HR-high watch may show 820–880. Planning number: ~700. Run sessions through the Running Calories Calculator. Math background: How Many Calories Does Running Burn?

HR Is the Quiet Culprit

Optical HR that reads high on “easy” days inflates calories; lag on intervals distorts both ways. A chest strap usually pulls estimates closer to MET — it does not remove model bias. See Garmin vs COROS HR accuracy.

Make Wearables Useful Anyway

Update body weight when you change ~2+ kg, prefer a chest strap on key workouts if calories inform eating, stick to one ecosystem for weekly sums, and recalibrate after heat waves or firmware jumps. Pace, distance, and HR zones remain the strong fields — calories are decision support.

Practical Takeaway

  1. Decide food from multi-week weight and performance
  2. Cross-check hard weeks with MET/distance math
  3. Use one watch’s active calories with a personal haircut when it exceeds MET by ~15%+

During a cut, default to the lower end of the MET–watch range. Rings closing are not receipts.

FAQ

Will a chest strap fix overestimation?

It usually reduces HR-driven error. It does not eliminate proprietary bias or active-vs-total confusion. Still compare to MET.

Should I eat back watch calories in a cut?

Generally no — or only a fraction after a 15–25% haircut when the watch beats MET. Plan the week, not the ring.
