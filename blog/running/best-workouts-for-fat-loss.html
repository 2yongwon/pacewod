Best Workouts for Fat Loss: Zone 2, Intervals, and Metcons Compared

Updated August 2026 · 4 min read

Fat loss is driven by a sustained energy deficit. Workouts help by adding calories, protecting muscle, and staying repeatable. Zone 2, intervals, and metcons each win on different axes — density, recoverability, and adherence. For zone mechanics, see Best Heart Rate for Fat Burn and the Zone 2 Running Guide.

Key Points

  • Rank by weekly value: (calories/session) × (sessions you’ll finish) × (muscle retained)
  • Zone 2: ~400–550 kcal per 50 min for many ~70 kg runners; low recovery cost; 3–6×/week realistic. Set HR with the Zone 2 Calculator
  • Intervals: higher density (~450–700 kcal/session) but 48+ hours recovery; dose 1–2×/week; keep hard running near ≤20% of weekly running time (Polarized 80/20)
  • Metcons: ~350–600 kcal for a hard 12–20 min piece + warm-up; help muscle retention; cap at 2–3 hard metcons/week in a cut — see CrossFit for Fat Loss
  • EPOC “afterburn” is real but usually modest (tens of kcal, not hundreds)

Pick by Time Budget

  • <2.5 h/week: 2 metcons + 1 interval/hill + 2 short strength sessions; optional 30 min Zone 2
  • 3–5 h/week: 60–75% minutes Zone 2; 1 run quality day; 2 metcon or 1 metcon + 2 strength; one rest day
  • 6+ h/week: Zone 2 is the calorie engine; cap hard days at 2–3 total

Estimate runs with the Running Calories Calculator; discount wearable metcon numbers ~10–20%.

Sample Week (~4.5 Hours)

  • MonStrength 40 min · optional 15 min easy bike
  • TueMetcon 12–16 min hard
  • WedZone 2 run 50–60 min
  • ThuIntervals: 6×2 min hard / 2 min easy (~45 min)
  • FriZone 2 40 min or rest if RHR +5 bpm
  • SatZone 2 long easy 70–80 min
  • SunRest · light walk

Practical Takeaway

Default: Zone 2 majority + 1–2 hard days. Minimal time: density (metcons + intervals) plus strength. Diet still sets the ceiling — a 300–500 kcal deficit with protein ~1.6–2.2 g/kg beats heroic HIIT at maintenance. If weight stalls 21 days, verify intake first, then add Zone 2, then consider one extra hard session.

FAQ

Is HIIT better than Zone 2 for belly fat?

No workout spot-reduces belly fat. HIIT burns more per minute; Zone 2 often wins weekly totals because you can repeat it. Sustain a moderate deficit with a mix you’ll keep.

Can I lose fat with Zone 2 only?

Yes — many athletes do well on 150–250 minutes Zone 2/week plus protein and two short strength sessions. Add intervals for race fitness or when time is too short for easy volume.
