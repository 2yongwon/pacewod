Best Workouts for Fat Loss: Zone 2, Intervals, or Metcons?

Updated August 2026 · 5 min read

The internet says HIIT melts fat fastest; your coach says Zone 2; the box says metcons. The conflict is maximizing calories per minute versus maximizing adherence and recovery while you actually stay in a deficit. The “best” workout is the one that raises weekly expenditure without wrecking tomorrow’s session.

What Each Modality Buys You

  • Zone 2: high recoverable volume, lower injury drama, easy to dose — set ceilings with the Zone 2 Calculator
  • Intervals: fitness and time-efficient burn, higher recovery cost — place 1–2×/week max while cutting
  • Metcons: dense effort and strength-endurance, appetite and joint stress can spike — scale loads

Fat loss still needs a calorie deficit (deficit guide). Use the Calories Calculator for run estimates, not as a metcon oracle. Strength work two days a week often retains muscle better than adding a third all-out conditioning piece.

Worked Scenario: ~4.5-Hour Week

  • Mon: Zone 2 run 50 min
  • Tue: Strength 40 min + short metcon 12 min (scaled)
  • Wed: Zone 2 run 40 min or rest if sore
  • Thu: Intervals 35–40 min total (including warm-up/cool-down)
  • Sat: Long Zone 2 75–90 min
  • Optional: easy walk NEAT on off days (NEAT guide)

If Thursday intervals and Tuesday metcons leave Saturday’s long run shuffling, demote one hard day to Zone 2. Hybrid context: running vs CrossFit.

Expert Note

Afterburn (EPOC) from intervals is real but usually small compared with total daily intake. Do not program your deficit around afterburn myths. Injury history, sleep, and skill level change what is “efficient.” Very low body-fat goals and aggressive training need professional coaching and medical awareness — not endless metcon PRs in a steep cut.

Time-Budget Decision Rule

<3 hours/week: mostly Zone 2 + 1 short hard session + steps. ~3–5 hours: Zone 2 base, 1 interval day, optional scaled metcon. >5 hours: keep intensity scarce; volume should stay mostly easy. If the deficit is slipping, fix food logging before adding another red-line day.

FAQ

Is Zone 2 too easy to lose fat?

No. Easy volume is often the most sustainable way to raise weekly burn while lifting and sleeping enough to keep the deficit.
