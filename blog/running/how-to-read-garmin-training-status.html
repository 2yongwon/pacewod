How to Read Garmin Training Status, Load, and Recovery Time

Updated August 2026 · 4 min read

Garmin Training Status compresses recent load, VO2 max trend, and recovery markers into a label: Productive, Maintaining, Peaking, Recovery, Unproductive, Overreaching, Detraining, and related states. It needs ~7–10 days of consistent HR-based activities to stabilize. It is a lagging check on your plan — not a daily crystal ball.

Labels That Matter

  • Productive: load rising, fitness responding — stay the course; do not add junk miles
  • Maintaining: flat load/fitness — fine on recovery/taper; investigate if you expected a build
  • Peaking: fitness high, load dropping — classic taper; resist “one more hard session”
  • Recovery / Overreaching: recent stress high vs fitness — prioritize easy/rest; confirm with RHR/HRV
  • Unproductive / Detraining: load without fitness gains, or load too low — audit easy-day compliance, HR accuracy, and whether quality is too sparse or life stress is high

Load, Recovery Time, Body Battery

Acute load vs chronic load drives many flags. Recovery Time is a countdown heuristic after hard sessions — useful, not absolute. Body Battery and HRV Status explain overnight recovery; alcohol and late meals move them as much as intervals. Clean HR inputs matter: wrist optical errors on intervals skew Training Effect — use a chest strap for key sessions (HR accuracy guide).

Watch vs Plan vs Feel

Follow the plan when status is Productive/Maintaining and you feel fine. Override the watch when local pain, illness, or RHR +5 bpm disagrees with a green label. Override panic when one Overreaching day follows a planned race — deload as scheduled. Easy runs should still respect the Zone 2 Calculator.

COROS Context

COROS EvoLab/Training Load answers a similar question with different branding. Same rule: trends + morning RHR + workout quality beat any single word on the dial. See RHR and recovery.

Practical Takeaway

Use Training Status weekly to validate periodization. Keep HR data clean, protect easy days, and let pain/illness veto green scores. Tools: VO2 Max Calculator, HR Zones Calculator.

FAQ

Why does status flip every day?

Usually a young baseline, missed wear at night, or noisy wrist HR. Wear the watch consistently for 10+ days before judging.

Should I rest whenever it says Overreaching?

Check RHR, sleep, and legs. Planned overload can flag Overreaching briefly; persistent flags plus poor sessions mean a real deload.
