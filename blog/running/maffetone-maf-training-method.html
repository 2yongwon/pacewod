Maffetone MAF Training Method

Updated August 2026 · 4 min read

MAF (Maximum Aerobic Function) caps easy running at a single heart rate from the 180−age formula. Same intent as Zone 2 — protect aerobic development — with simpler math and stricter ceiling discipline.

The 180−Age Formula

Base MAF HR = 180 − age, then adjust:

  • Subtract 10 if recovering from illness/injury or on meds affecting HR (common MAF guidance)
  • Subtract 5 if sick often, inconsistent, or returning from a break
  • No change if training consistently 2+ years without problems
  • Add 5 only if competing regularly for 2+ years with progress and no issues

Example: age 40, consistent → MAF 140 bpm. That is often near the top of a Karvonen Zone 2 band, not the middle. Compare with the Zone 2 Calculator and LTHR vs Max HR.

How to Train With MAF

  • All aerobic runs at or below MAF HR; walk hills rather than spike
  • Use a reliable HR monitor (chest strap preferred; see watch HR accuracy)
  • Monthly MAF test: timed distance or fixed route at exactly MAF HR — track pace improvement
  • Watch drift/decoupling on longer MAF runs

MAF vs LTHR Zone 2

  • MAF: one ceiling, zero equipment beyond HR, great for gray-zone addicts; can be conservative if you’re aerobically strong
  • LTHR Zone 2: range tied to your threshold (e.g. ~85–89% LTHR); usually better individualized once you field-test

Neither replaces max-HR factory watch defaults without you entering real numbers. See also Zone 2 Guide and Easy Pace Guide.

Who It Fits

Good for beginners, return-to-run, and runners who cannot stay easy. Less ideal alone if you need frequent race-specific speed or already have a solid LTHR-based system.

Progress Signals

Improving: monthly test pace at MAF HR gets faster; resting HR stable or trending down; less late-run drift. Stalling: pace flat for 6–8 weeks despite consistency — check sleep, fueling, or whether optical HR is capping you artificially low. MAF is a ceiling, not a target to sit on every minute if true easy is lower.

Practical Takeaway

Use MAF as a strict easy ceiling and prove progress with monthly pace-at-MAF tests. When you outgrow a single number, move to LTHR-anchored Zone 2 ranges — keep the honesty, refine the math.
