HRV for Runners: What Garmin, COROS, and Apple Watch Measure — and When to Rest

Updated August 2026 · 4 min read

HRV (beat-to-beat variation) tracks autonomic balance. Relative to your baseline, higher usually means more recovered; lower often tracks load, poor sleep, alcohol, or illness. Absolute numbers across athletes mislead. Pair HRV with resting heart rate and sleep.

Key Points

  • Prefer overnight trends (7–14 day baseline) over one morning reading
  • RMSSD-family metrics dominate most readiness scores; stay on one device mid-block
  • ±1 SD rule for hard days: if overnight HRV is >1 SD below your 14-day mean and RHR is up or sleep was short → swap intervals/tempo for Zone 2 or rest
  • Easy volume is more forgiving — keep it truly easy with the Zone 2 calculator
  • Garmin: HRV Status + Training Status together are more useful than either alone (Training Status guide)
  • COROS: recovery/load narrative; less daily green/yellow theater
  • Apple Watch: needs apps (Athlytic, etc.) for runner-facing readiness; charging can cut overnight samples
  • Alcohol, travel, watch fit, and menstrual phase all move HRV without a fitness change

Decision Framework

  1. HRV within ±1 SD → run the plan
  2. One morning −1 to −1.5 SD, warm-up OK → proceed or trim intensity 5–10%
  3. −1 SD for 2+ mornings, or −1 SD + RHR +5 bpm → skip/replace hard work
  4. Green HRV is not a license to stack an unplanned second hard session

Example: mean RMSSD 55 ms, SD 7 → ~48 ms is −1 SD. Two mornings at 42–44 with RHR +7 → cancel Thursday intervals. Deeper fatigue patterns: overtraining signs.

Common mistakes: chasing a friend’s absolute HRV number, skipping every yellow morning, or ignoring rising resting HR because the readiness icon is green. Context beats the badge.

Practical Takeaway

HRV earns its keep as a trend tool that catches slides before dead-legged quality sessions. Require aligned signals (HRV + RHR/sleep/feel) before rewriting the week.

FAQ

Garmin unbalanced — skip everything?

No. Unbalanced after a hard block is expected. Chronic unbalanced with rising load and poor sleep is the warning. Soften quality; keep easy aerobic if legs feel fine.

Can I compare Apple HRV to Garmin?

Not directly. Methods and metrics differ. Recalibrate 7–10 nights after any device switch.
