How to Find and Run Your Easy Pace

Updated August 2026 · 5 min read

Calculator easy pace says 5:30/km. Heat pushes HR to 156 at that speed. One number is ego; the other is cost. Easy pace is the session most runners botch — too fast and hard workouts never stick. Aim for conversational effort and let heart rate override a fixed pace when conditions change.

Three Anchors (Use Two)

  1. Heart rate: Zone 2 via the Zone 2 Calculator or HR Zones Calculator.
  2. Talk test: full sentences without gasping.
  3. Race-relative pace: ~1–1.5 min/mile slower than 5K; seed from the Pace Calculator, then adjust for heat/hills.

Worked Scenario: 22:30 5K Runner

Recent 5K 22:30 → calculator easy ~5:20–5:40/km; Zone 2 HR ~138–150 bpm. Cool flat day at 5:35/km and 142 bpm: keep it. Hot humid day at the same pace and 156 bpm: slow to ~5:55/km or add walk breaks until HR returns under 150. HR reflects cost; pace is only an output. Gray-zone creep week after week? Read Zone 2 vs Zone 3.

Expert Note

Do not trust Garmin/COROS “suggested easy pace” after weeks of hard running — those models often normalize an inflated easy pace. Also treat COROS pace-zone 2 and HR-zone 2 as different tools on hills or heat; they will disagree.

By Training Phase

  • Base: every easy run stays Zone 2 — pace is irrelevant
  • Build: easy days still capped; only planned long-run finish segments go moderate
  • Taper: easy pace may feel fast at lower HR — don’t race the recovery jog

Treadmill: 1–2% incline is fine; still cap HR rather than chasing outdoor pace numbers. Deeper dive: Zone 2 Running Guide.

Social and Watch Pressure

Group runs and Strava segments quietly raise “easy” pace until HR is Zone 3. State the ceiling before you start, or run alone on recovery days. If a partner’s easy is your tempo, split after the warm-up rather than absorbing their gray zone for an hour.

Decision Rule

If heat index is high, resting HR is elevated, or yesterday was hard — the HR ceiling wins over calculator pace. If the day is cool, flat, and resting HR is normal, both talk test and HR must pass before you hold a target pace.
