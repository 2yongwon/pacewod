How to Find and Run Your Easy Pace

Updated August 2026 · 4 min read

Easy pace is the session most runners botch. Too fast and hard workouts never stick; slightly “too slow” still builds fitness. Aim for conversational effort — often 60–90 sec/mile slower than 5K pace — and let heart rate override ego when conditions change.

Three Ways to Find It

  1. Heart rate (primary): Zone 2, roughly 60–70% max HR or Karvonen equivalent. Use the Zone 2 Calculator or HR Zones Calculator.
  2. Talk test: full sentences without gasping.
  3. Race-relative pace: ~1–1.5 min/mile slower than 5K; 45–75 sec/mile slower than 10K; 30–60 sec/mile slower than half. Seed from the Pace Calculator, then adjust for heat/hills.

When Pace and HR Disagree

Example: 5K 22:30 → calculator easy ~5:20–5:40/km; Zone 2 HR ~138–150 bpm. Cool flat day at 5:35/km and 142 bpm is fine. Hot humid day at the same pace and 156 bpm is Zone 3 — slow to ~5:55/km or walk breaks. HR reflects cost; pace is only an output. Drift into gray zone week after week? Read Zone 2 vs Zone 3.

Common Mistakes

  • Ego pacing with faster partners on recovery days
  • Chasing a fixed pace when sleep, heat, or stress raise cost
  • Trusting Garmin/COROS “easy” suggestions after weeks of hard running (they normalize inflated easy pace)
  • Treating COROS pace-zone 2 and HR-zone 2 as identical on hills or heat

Decision Rules

  • Heat index high, resting HR elevated, or day after hard work → HR cap wins
  • Flat, cool, normal resting HR → talk test and HR both must pass
  • Downhill/tailwind → ignore fast pace spikes; keep HR ceiling

By Training Phase

  • Base: every easy run stays Zone 2 — pace is irrelevant
  • Build: easy days still capped; only planned long-run finish segments go moderate
  • Taper: easy pace may feel fast at lower HR (freshness) — don’t race the recovery jog

Treadmill: 1–2% incline is fine for easy work; still cap HR rather than chasing outdoor pace numbers.

Practical Takeaway

Protect easy days like quality sessions. Cap HR, accept slower pace when needed, and expect easy pace to quicken over 4–6 weeks of honest volume. Deeper dive: Zone 2 Running Guide.
