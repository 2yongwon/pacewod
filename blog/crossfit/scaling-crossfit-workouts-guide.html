How to Scale CrossFit Workouts: Load, Volume, and Skill Substitutions

Updated August 2026 · 5 min read

Rx pride and intended stimulus often conflict. A scaled Fran in 4–7 minutes with near-unbroken sets trains the sprint Fran was meant to be; a 14-minute Rx grind of singles trains a long chipper instead. Scaling is load management with a stopwatch — not a lesser workout.

After you pick loads, pace with the AMRAP, For Time, and Benchmark guides. Build scaled sessions in the WOD Generator.

Stimulus First

Name the intended stimulus in one sentence, then scale to finish in that time domain at the right relative intensity.

  • Short For Time (Fran/Isabel): finish within ~1.5–2× elite; intermediate Fran often 4–7 min. Projected >10 min → scale load/skill first
  • AMRAP 12–20: round 1 at RPE 5–6; if round 1 is RPE 8, scale before the clock
  • EMOM: keep 10–20 sec rest/min; cut reps 20–30% before cutting rest

Three Gates (Fail Any → Scale)

  1. Competence: Can you do ≥50% of Rx volume unbroken when fresh (to standard)?
  2. Relative load: Short cycling metcons ≈40–55% of 1RM; longer (15–40 min) ≈30–45%.
  3. Projected finish: One practice round × rounds + 10–15% fatigue.

Numeric scenario: Fran thrusters at 95 lb. Front-squat 1RM 135 lb → 95 is ~70% (strength set, not cycling). Drop to 55–65 lb (~40–48%). Athlete A: 95 lb singles, finishes 12:40. Athlete B: 65 lb in 8-7-6 / 8-7 / unbroken 9, finishes 5:50 — closer to Fran’s intended stimulus.

Expert Note

Mixed scaling is legitimate: Rx thrusters + banded pull-ups, or lighter bar + Rx gymnastics. Log the scale honestly (“Fran 75/55 banded”) so retests compare like with like.

Decision rule: load first when relative intensity is too high or you can’t do ~40–50% of a set unbroken fresh; volume first when load % is right but rests exceed work by midpoint; skill sub first when the movement isn’t safe to standard.

Load, Volume, Gymnastics

  • Duration heuristics: <8 min 40–50% 1RM · 8–15 min 35–50% · 15–30 min 30–40% · 30–60+ min 25–35%
  • Volume: keep ratios (21-15-9 → 15-12-9); cut 20–40% on the limiter; chippers cut each station 25–40%
  • Pull-ups: C2B/kip → banded → ring rows — not burpees by default
  • HSPU: Rx → box/pike → floor pike or DB press (return from injury)

Don’t scale strength days, fresh skill practice, or sessions where all three gates pass. Advance one variable at a time (+5–10 lb, one skill tier, or +10–20% volume) after two clean sessions in the target window.

Practical Takeaway

Scale so time domain and density match the whiteboard’s intent. Prefer 65/45 unbroken over 95/65 in sets of three when the goal is a short couplet.

FAQ

Is scaled “less effective” than Rx?

No — hitting the intended window at the right intensity beats an Rx grind. Retest the same scale.
