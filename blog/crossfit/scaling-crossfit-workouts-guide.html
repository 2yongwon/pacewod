How to Scale CrossFit Workouts: Load, Volume, and Skill Substitutions

Updated August 2026 · 4 min read

Scaling is load management with a stopwatch. Rx is a hypothesis — test it against movement quality, intended stimulus, and recovery. A scaled Fran in 4–7 minutes with near-unbroken sets beats a 14-minute Rx grind of singles.

After you pick loads, pace with the AMRAP, For Time, and Benchmark guides. Build scaled sessions in the WOD Generator.

Stimulus First

Name the intended stimulus in one sentence, then scale to finish in that time domain at the right relative intensity. If Rx Fran means 12+ minutes of thruster singles, you are training a long chipper — drop load until sets of 5–10 move.

  • Short For Time (Fran/Isabel): finish within ~1.5–2× elite; intermediate Fran often 4–7 min. Projected >10 min → scale load/skill first
  • AMRAP 12–20: round 1 at RPE 5–6; if round 1 is RPE 8, scale before the clock
  • EMOM: keep 10–20 sec rest/min; cut reps 20–30% before cutting rest

Three Gates (Fail Any → Scale)

  1. Competence: Can you do ≥50% of Rx volume unbroken when fresh (to standard)?
  2. Relative load: Short cycling metcons ≈40–55% of 1RM; longer (15–40 min) ≈30–45%. Fran thrusters at 95 lb need roughly that band of front-squat 1RM — 135 lb FS makes 95 lb a strength set → 55–65 lb.
  3. Projected finish: One practice round × rounds + 10–15% fatigue. If Diane 9+9 already takes 3 minutes as singles, scale HSPU before starting.

Load, Volume, Gymnastics

  • Duration heuristics: <8 min 40–50% 1RM · 8–15 min 35–50% · 15–30 min 30–40% · 30–60+ min 25–35%
  • Volume cuts: preserve ratios (21-15-9 → 15-12-9 or 15-9-6); cut 20–40% on the limiter; chippers cut each station 25–40% (half Murph > delete stations)
  • Pull-ups: C2B/kip → banded → ring rows (30–45°) — not burpees by default
  • HSPU: Rx → box/pike elevated → floor pike or DB press (injury return)

Prefer 65/45 unbroken over 95/65 in sets of three. Mixed scaling is fine: Rx thrusters + banded pull-ups. Don’t scale strength days, fresh skill practice, or sessions where all three gates pass.

Practical Takeaway

Scale so time domain and density match the whiteboard’s intent. Advance one variable at a time (+5–10 lb, one skill tier, or +10–20% volume) after two clean sessions in the target window.

FAQ

Scale load or volume first?

Load first when relative intensity is too high or you can’t do ~40–50% of a set unbroken fresh. Volume first when load % is right but rests exceed work by midpoint. Skill sub first when the movement isn’t safe/standard.

Is scaled “less effective” than Rx?

No — hitting the intended window at the right intensity beats an Rx grind. Log scales honestly (“Fran 75/55 banded”) and retest the same scale.
