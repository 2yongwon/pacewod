Barbell Cycling in CrossFit: Touch-and-Go, Singles, and Grip Management

Updated August 2026 · 5 min read

Touch-and-go looks faster; singles look safer — the conflict is %1RM and what comes next. Strength alone doesn’t fix cycling under fatigue. Breathing, bounce timing, and break strategy do. Pair with For Time, Chipper, and Benchmark guides. Practice: WOD Generator.

Touch-and-Go vs Singles

  • ≤45% 1RM and positions hold fresh → default TnG; one planned break on 21s
  • 45–60% → small TnG (3–5) or fast singles with 1–2 breath resets
  • >60% or positions fail → singles from rep 1

Numeric scenario (Fran thrusters): Fresh cycle ~1.5–2.0 sec/rep. Intermediate plan: 12-9 on 21s, 8-7 or unbroken 15s, unbroken 9s → often 4:30–7:00 with pull-ups. Unbroken 21 that strands you for 40 seconds before pull-ups usually loses to 12-9 with 8-sec breaks. If first 5 thrusters take 12+ sec (≈2.4/rep), scale load. Weaker pull-ups → break thrusters earlier (7-7-7) to keep pull-ups in sets of 5–8.

Expert Note

Isabel: TnG only if load ≤~50% snatch 1RM with locked receives. Example: snatch 1RM 185 → Rx 135 is ~73% (singles or scale). At 95 (~51%), sets of 5–6 with ~5-sec breaks often beat ugly TnG with misses.

Decision rule: choose TnG vs singles by %1RM and position quality; if you need 30+ sec rest after five “metcon” reps, scale — don’t invent a new cycling style mid-WOD.

Breaks, Grip, Practice

  • Sets of 21: 12-9 or 8-7-6 · 15: unbroken or 8-7 · 9–10: prefer unbroken
  • Break 1–2 reps before technical failure; rests 5–12 sec
  • Hook grip on cleans/snatches; chalk on planned breaks; open hands between stations
  • Chipper DLs: plan 10s (not unbroken 50). Deadlift TnG caps often 5–10 unless ≤40% 1RM
  • EMOM 10: 5–8 thrusters at Fran load (≥20 sec rest; cut if <15 sec by min 6)

Hard cycling metcons 2–3×/week max; light technique more often. Practice piece: 21-15-9 power cleans at ≤45% 1RM touch-and-go to rehearse break timing without Fran’s pull-up interference.

Practical Takeaway

Judge success by total workout time and next-movement quality — not bar time alone. Protect grip for what follows. Unbroken isn’t a virtue if positions fail or rest after five reps balloons past 30 seconds.

FAQ

Is TnG always faster?

Only when positions stay crisp and small-set rests stay under ~12 sec. Above ~60% 1RM, disciplined singles usually win.
