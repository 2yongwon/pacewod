Barbell Cycling in CrossFit: Touch-and-Go, Singles, and Grip Management

Updated August 2026 · 4 min read

Barbell cycling is moving a light-to-moderate bar under fatigue without technique or grip collapse. Strength alone doesn’t fix it — breathing, bounce timing, and break strategy do.

Pair with For Time, Chipper, and Benchmark guides. Practice pieces: WOD Generator.

Touch-and-Go vs Singles

  • ≤45% 1RM and positions hold fresh → default TnG; one planned break on 21s
  • 45–60% → small TnG (3–5) or fast singles with 1–2 breath resets
  • >60% or positions fail → singles from rep 1

Unbroken isn’t a virtue. 12-9 thrusters with 8-sec breaks and clean pull-ups often beat unbroken 21s that strand you for 40 seconds.

Fran and Isabel Math

Fran thrusters: ~1.5–2.0 sec/rep when fresh. Intermediate plan: 12-9 on 21s, 8-7 or unbroken 15s, unbroken 9s → often 4:30–7:00 with pull-ups. If first 5 thrusters take 12+ sec (≈2.4/rep), scale load. Weaker pull-ups → break thrusters earlier (e.g., 7-7-7) to keep pull-ups in sets of 5–8.

Isabel: TnG only if load ≤50% snatch 1RM with locked receives. At 50–65%, fast singles (~3–4 sec/rep including reset). Example: snatch 1RM 185 → Rx 135 is 73% (singles or scale); at 95 (51%), 6×5 with 5-sec breaks ≈ competitive intermediate window.

Breaks and Grip

  • Sets of 21: 12-9 or 8-7-6 · 15: unbroken or 8-7 · 9–10: prefer unbroken
  • Break 1–2 reps before technical failure; rests 5–12 sec — if you need 30+ sec after five “metcon” reps, scale
  • Hook grip on cleans/snatches; chalk on planned breaks only; open hands between stations
  • Chipper DLs: plan 10s (not unbroken 50). Deadlift TnG caps often 5–10 unless ≤40% 1RM

Practice Templates

  • EMOM 10: 5–8 thrusters at Fran load or −10 lb (≥20 sec rest; cut reps if <15 sec by min 6)
  • 21-15-9 TnG: power cleans ≤45% 1RM
  • Hard cycling metcons 2–3×/week max; light technique more often

Practical Takeaway

Choose TnG vs singles by %1RM and position quality. Plan breaks before failure, protect grip for what follows, and judge success by total workout time — not bar time alone.

FAQ

Is TnG always faster?

Only when positions stay crisp and small-set rests stay under ~12 sec. Above ~60% 1RM, disciplined singles usually beat ugly TnG with misses.

How should I break 30 Isabel snatches?

TnG: sets of 5–6 with ~5-sec breaks. Singles: drop, reset, one breath, pull (~3–4 sec/rep). Avoid 15 unbroken then death-march singles.
