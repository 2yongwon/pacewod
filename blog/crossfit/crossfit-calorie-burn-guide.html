How Many Calories Does a CrossFit Workout Burn? Metcon Estimates vs Watch Data

Updated August 2026 · 5 min read

Your watch says 700 kcal for a 12-minute Fran; MET math says closer to 100–200 on the clock. That conflict is the point — CrossFit burn is real, but Garmin/Apple totals optimized for continuous locomotion often inflate mixed metcons. Treat the screenshot as an upper bound, not a meal voucher.

Steady-state running math: How Many Calories Does Running Burn?. Repeatable sessions: WOD Generator. Daily planning aid: Calories Calculator.

MET Formula (70 kg)

kcal/min ≈ (MET × kg × 3.5) / 200

  • 8 MET ≈ 9.8 kcal/min · 10 MET ≈ 12.25 · 12 MET ≈ 14.7 · 14 MET ≈ 17.15
  • Vigorous circuits often average ~8–10 MET once rests and transitions count

Numeric scenario: Fran ~7:00 at ~12 MET → ≈103 kcal on the clock. Add 5–8 min warm-up at ~5 MET → often ~150–250 kcal for the hard block — not 600. Cindy 20 min at 10 MET ≈245 kcal (9–11 MET band ≈220–300 before warm-up). Murph-style ~60 min: run portions ~20–25 min at ~10 MET ≈245–305 + bodyweight block ~35–40 min at ~8–9 MET ≈275–350 → session ≈550–700 kcal for many 70 kg athletes. Faster finishes can burn less total because the clock is shorter.

Expert Note

EPOC after 10–20 min hard work is commonly on the order of tens of calories (~30–80), not hundreds. Add ~5–10% to in-session MET estimates — not 50%. When watch “active calories” exceed MET midpoints by a wide margin, haircut CrossFit totals ~15–25% for planning.

Decision rule: plan with MET ranges; if the watch exceeds the MET midpoint by >25% on a mixed metcon, use the haircut for weekly energy math — validate with 14-day weight/performance trends, not one screenshot.

Weekly Math Matters More

Four × 15-min AMRAPs at ~10 MET ≈735 kcal + one 40-min easy run ≈250 → ~985 training kcal/week. Two metcons + three × 45-min Zone 2 often ≈1,400+. Intensity can feel higher in the metcon week while weekly burn favors hybrid/aerobic volume.

Practical Takeaway

Fran ~100–200 clocked; Cindy-style ~220–300; Murph-style ~550–700+ (70 kg ranges). Use wearables for trends, not nutrition gospel.

FAQ

Does CrossFit burn more than running?

Per minute, hard metcons can match/exceed easy running. Per session and week, running often wins on duration. A 12-min 10-MET AMRAP ≈147 kcal vs ~350+ for a 50-min easy run (70 kg).
