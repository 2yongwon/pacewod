How Many Calories Does a CrossFit Workout Burn? Metcon Estimates vs Watch Data

Updated August 2026 · 4 min read

Watch screenshots of 500–900 kcal for a 12–20 minute metcon are often inflated. CrossFit burn is real — treat Garmin/Apple totals as upper bounds, not nutrition gospel.

Steady-state running math: How Many Calories Does Running Burn?. Repeatable sessions: WOD Generator.

MET Formula (70 kg Examples)

kcal/min = (MET × kg × 3.5) / 200

  • 8 MET ≈ 9.8 kcal/min · 10 MET ≈ 12.25 · 12 MET ≈ 14.7 · 14 MET ≈ 17.15
  • Vigorous circuit averages often ~8–10 MET once rests/transitions count

Fran ~7:00 at ~12 MET: ≈103 kcal on the clock (+ warm-up 5–8 min at ~5 MET) → often ~150–250 kcal for the hard block — not 600.

Cindy 20 min at 10 MET: ≈245 kcal (9–11 MET band ≈220–300 before warm-up).

Murph-style ~60 min: run portions ~20–25 min at ~10 MET ≈245–305; bodyweight block ~35–40 min at ~8–9 MET ≈275–350; session ≈550–700 kcal. Faster finishes can burn less total because the clock is shorter.

EPOC and Watch Inflation

Afterburn after 10–20 min hard work is commonly ~30–80 kcal — add ~5–10% to in-session MET estimates, not 50%. Watches optimized for continuous locomotion misread chalk breaks, kipping, and HR lag; haircut active calories ~15–25% when they exceed MET estimates. Chest straps help; they don’t make proprietary models perfect.

Weekly Math Matters More

Four × 15-min AMRAPs at ~10 MET ≈735 kcal + one 40-min easy run ≈250 → ~985 training kcal/week. Two metcons + three × 45-min Zone 2 ≈1,420. Intensity can feel higher in the metcon week while weekly burn favors hybrid/aerobic volume. Pair hard WODs with easy aerobic work when fat loss is primary.

Practical Takeaway

Plan with MET ranges (Fran ~100–200 clocked; Cindy-style ~220–300; Murph-style ~550–700+ for 70 kg). Use wearables for trends, haircut CrossFit totals 15–25%, and validate with 14-day body-composition outcomes — not one “812 kcal” screenshot.

FAQ

Does CrossFit burn more than running?

Per minute, hard metcons can match/exceed easy running. Per session and week, running often wins on duration. A 12-min 10-MET AMRAP ≈147 kcal vs ~350+ for a 50-min easy run (70 kg).

Eat back watch calories?

No. Prefer MET midpoints, apply a haircut to inflated totals, and track weekly weight/performance.
