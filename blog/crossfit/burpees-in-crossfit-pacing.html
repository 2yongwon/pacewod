Burpees in CrossFit: Efficient Movement and Sustainable Sets

Updated August 2026 · 4 min read

Burpees are simple and merciless. The athletes who score well aren’t the ones who sprint the first 20 — they’re the ones who keep a rhythm for the last 20. Efficiency and set math beat thrashing. Build sessions with the WOD Generator; pair with AMRAP or EMOM formats.

Key Points

  • Step-back or jump-back — pick one you can repeat without form collapse
  • Chest-to-deck and full extension when the standard requires it; soft reps waste time in no-reps
  • Breathe on a pattern (e.g., exhale on the stand) so sets don’t become panic breathing
  • Plan breaks before you need them; unbroken ego fails after minute five
  • Scale volume before deleting the movement entirely — see scaling guide

Efficient Movement

  1. Hands plant under shoulders; jump or step feet back without a long pause in plank.
  2. Chest contacts the floor (or target); keep hips from sagging into a banana.
  3. Feet land outside hands when jumping in; stand tall — hips/knees open if required.
  4. Optional small hop: only if it doesn’t cost more than it saves.

Step-back burpees often win long chippers. Jump-back wins short sprints if your landing stays quiet. Related skill work: gymnastics skills.

Sustainable Sets

  • 10–20 repsOften unbroken or 10-10 with 3–5 breaths
  • 30–50 repsSets of 8–12; fixed rest (3–5 breaths) beats random staring
  • 100+Steady singles or doubles with a metronome rhythm — no early 20s

In couplets (e.g., burpees + thrusters), leave the burpee set when you can still pick unbroken barbell work

Quick Templates

  • EMOM 10: 8–12 burpees · leave ≥12 sec · drop reps if rest vanishes
  • AMRAP 12: 10 burpees + 12 wall balls · burpees in 2×5 after round 3 if needed
  • For Time: 50 burpees — open at a pace you can hold ±2 sec/rep through 40–50

Practical Takeaway

Choose a movement version you can repeat, lock a breathing rhythm, and break on purpose. Smooth reps compound; heroic early sets do not. Browse more in the Workout Library.

FAQ

Step-back or jump-back?

Use step-back for long sets and when heart rate spikes kill the next movement. Jump-back for short, scored sprints if landings stay controlled.

How do I stop dying after 15 reps?

Slow the stand slightly, exhale on every rep, and switch to planned sets of 5–8 with short fixed rests instead of going unbroken until failure.
