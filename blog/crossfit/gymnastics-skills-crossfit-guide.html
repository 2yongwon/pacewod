Gymnastics Skills for CrossFit: Pull-Ups, HSPU, Muscle-Ups, and Progressions

Updated August 2026 · 4 min read

CrossFit gymnastics fails more athletes on volume than talent. Build prerequisites, progression gates, and volume caps under low fatigue — then expose skills to metcons.

Scale with How to Scale. Practice density via EMOMs. Sessions: WOD Generator.

Pull-Ups: Strict → Kip → Butterfly

  • Strict gate: 3–5 unbroken chin-over-bar, ≤3 sec eccentric. Build with scap pulls, banded/negatives, ring rows, dead hangs 20–40 sec. ~40–60 quality strict reps/session, 2–3×/week.
  • Kip gate: hollow↔arch swing first; then 8–12 unbroken kip. Cadence ≈1.0–1.3 sec/rep. EMOM 8–10: 3–5 kips + hollow (≥25 sec rest).
  • Butterfly: after comfortable 15+ kips; train sets of 3–5 for weeks. Metcon use: 15+ unbroken fresh with full chin height. C2B only after easy 15–20 chin-over-bar kips.

If you can’t active-hang 30–40 sec, delay high-volume kipping — use ring rows/bands in WODs.

HSPU and Muscle-Ups

HSPU ladder: pike 3×8–12 → elevated pike → wall walks + nose-to-wall holds → box HSPU → strict singles → kip. New trainees: ~20–30 quality HSPU/session first month. Diane: if fresh capacity is 5, open with small sets (5-5-5-3-3); budget 4–6 min for 45 HSPU with breaks — or scale.

Bar MU prereqs: 5–8 strict or 10–15 solid kip/C2B + 8–10 dips + competent hip pop. Rings: add false-grip work, 15–20 sec support, 5+ ring dips. Workout inclusion: 1–3 unbroken fresh; else pull+dip supersets. Skill days: 10–20 quality attempts max.

T2B: knee raise → KTE → T2B. Large volumes need 10–15 unbroken fresh; else sets of 3–5 or knees-to-chest.

Weekly Volume Budgets

  • Pulling (all hang): intermediates ≈150–250 quality reps/week; beginners 80–150. Murph week: cut other pulling ~50%+
  • HSPU/vertical press gymnastics: 40–80 learning; 80–120 established
  • MU attempts: 30–50/week including drills while developing
  • Hard hanging days ≤3×/week; 48 hr apart if elbows sensitive

Skill practice should sit Zone 1–2. Order: warm-up → skill (fresh) → strength → scaled metcon.

Practical Takeaway

Strict before dynamic, positions before volume, fresh practice before fatigued tests. Cap hanging/pressing so tendons keep up — progress gates beat random kip volume.

FAQ

Butterfly in every workout?

No. Use it for high-volume unbroken sets when timing is reliable. Prefer regular kip when fatigued or timing desyncs.

Elbows hurt after T2B/pull-ups?

Usually cumulative tendon load. Cut hanging 40–50% for 1–2 weeks, keep pain-free strict work, space hanging 48 hr. Sharp joint pain → get it assessed.
