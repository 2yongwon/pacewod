Partner WODs: Synchro, You-Go-I-Go, and Shared Work Strategy

Updated August 2026 · 4 min read

Partner WODs share a clock and rules that punish poor communication faster than poor fitness. Synchro shares timing; You-Go-I-Go shares pacing; shared chippers share decisions.

Build with the WOD Generator. Remixing solos: chippers, AMRAPs, For Time.

Synchro

Reps advance only when both hit standards on the same cycle. Set cadence to the slower sustainable athlete. Count the first 5–10 reps out loud. If one breaks, both break. Plan pauses every 8–12 synchro wall balls — failure resets both.

You-Go-I-Go Math

Rest is part of the work; sloppy handoffs erase it.

  • Heavy/technical: sets of 5–8
  • Smooth cycling: 10–15
  • Cals/meters: fixed chunks (10/8 cal, 100m) — not “until tired”

Front-loading 20 pull-ups then stranding your partner usually loses to even splits with fast switches. Agree who touches the bar first and whether chalk is allowed mid-station.

Shared Chippers and Mismatch

Assign limiters honestly (better pull-ups take more pull-ups). Prefer interruptible 10s/15s. Protect the next shared weakness. Uneven 60/40 on a strength station beats 50/50 pride. Scale load/target in synchro so cycle times can match. Stronger athlete: pace-set and stay emotionally neutral — don’t steal all the “fun” stations.

Tiny language: “Switch,” “Last two,” “Break,” “Short” (pre-agreed cut), “Push,” “Standards.”

Sample Templates

  • Synchro AMRAP 12: 8 synchro WB · 8 synchro burpees · 16 cal row YGIG 8/8
  • YGIG For Time: 100 pull-ups / push-ups / squats — pull-ups 5–7, push-ups 10, squats 20
  • Shared chipper: 100 cal · 80 swings · 60 box jumps · 40 pull-ups · 20 synchro burpees — small sets on pulling; cadence on final synchro

Place partner days like hard metcons. Hybrid athletes: avoid burpee-run partner chippers the day before a quality run (Running + CrossFit Balance).

Practical Takeaway

Agree cadence, set sizes, and switch language before 3-2-1-GO. Fast legal handoffs beat heroic early sets that strand the team in the middle third.

FAQ

Synchro vs YGIG?

Synchro = shared cadence so both must finish each rep together. YGIG = one works while the other recovers; handoffs set rest.

Why do we die mid partner AMRAP?

Usually oversized early sets, slow handoffs, or cadence set to the faster athlete. Shrink sets and talk earlier — round one is data for the plan you already wrote.
