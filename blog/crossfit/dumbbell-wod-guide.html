Dumbbell WODs: Unilateral Strength, Scaling Loads, and Metcon Templates

Updated August 2026 · 4 min read

A pair of dumbbells covers cleans, snatches, thrusters, lunges, rows, and carries in a small footprint. Unilateral loading exposes left-right gaps a barbell can hide.

Pair with EMOMs and AMRAPs, or build DB metcons in the WOD Generator.

Load Guidelines (Per Hand)

  • Light / high-rep: ~10–15% bodyweight
  • Moderate metcon (8–15): ~15–20% BW
  • Heavy metcon (5–8): ~20–25% BW
  • Single-DB (goblet/one-arm snatch): ~20–35% BW in the working hand

Examples: 140 lb athlete → thrusters ~20 lb/hand in AMRAP 12; 200 lb → ~35 lb. Two 50s ≠ “100 lb thruster” — expect DB thruster loads around 40–55% of your barbell thruster metcon weight for similar sets. Fran-style DB thrusters often start ~12–18% BW/hand (180 lb athlete ≈25–35 lb), not 50s.

If you can’t complete the first set in the planned break scheme when fresh, drop 5–10 lb/hand before the clock.

Unilateral Fatigue

Weak side dictates pace. Lead with the weak side; match rest to the slow side. If one side can’t do ≥80% of the other’s unbroken set, reduce load. Example: 50 alternating snatches — planned 6–7 per side beats “unbroken until left dies” by 30–45 sec for many athletes.

Six Concise Templates

  • Beginner AMRAP 10: 8 DL + 8 hang clean + 8 push press (~10–12% BW) · 5–7 rounds · 80–100 sec/round
  • Beginner EMOM 12: goblet squats / single-arm rows / core · ≥15 sec rest
  • Intermediate AMRAP 12: 10 thrusters + 12 reverse lunges (~15–18% BW) · 6–8 rounds · thrusters 6-4
  • Intermediate For Time: 21-15-9 alt snatches + burpee-over-DB (~18–22% BW) · 7–10 min
  • Advanced EMOM 16: odd devil’s press · even front squats · ≥12 sec rest
  • Advanced AMRAP 20: 15 hang C2OH + 20m farmer + 10 burpees (~20% BW) · 5–7 rounds

Scale order: load −5–10 lb → reps −20% → range (hang/push press) if positions break. Two DB metcons/week is enough alongside lifting/running — avoid thruster AMRAPs the day before heavy squats or long runs.

Practical Takeaway

Pick one load, 2–4 movements, 8–20 minute domains, and let the weak side set the scheme. Density and positions beat matching barbell tonnage.

FAQ

Are DB WODs enough without a barbell?

For conditioning and unilateral strength, yes for long stretches. Add a barbell when max strength or Olympic lifts become goals.

Only light DBs?

Raise density: higher reps, longer AMRAPs, slower eccentrics, or add burpees/lunges/runs between sets.
