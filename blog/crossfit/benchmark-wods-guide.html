CrossFit Benchmark WODs: Fran, Cindy, Murph, and How to Pace Them

Updated August 2026 · 4 min read

Benchmarks are repeatable tests — same standards, comparable scores months apart. They diagnose engine, gymnastics, and barbell cycling better than random novelty WODs. Train between tests with EMOMs, the WOD Generator, and Workout Library. Running balance: guide. Pace tools: Pace Calculator.

Key Points

  • Fran (21-15-9 thrusters + pull-ups): sprint benchmark — break before failure; thruster sets decide most scores
  • Cindy (20 min AMRAP 5 PU / 10 push-ups / 15 air squats): steady grind — even rounds beat a hot open
  • Murph (1 mi + 100/200/300 + 1 mi, vest optional): partition thoughtfully; opening mile controlled
  • Other classics (Helen, Grace, Diane, etc.) follow the same rule: retest under identical standards
  • Retest every 8–12 weeks; train the limiter between tests; do not use benchmarks as daily conditioning
  • Log scale, vest, and partitioning so future comparisons are honest

Murph Pacing Snapshot

  • Start1 mile run — controlled, not sprint (7:00–9:00/mi pace for most)
  • Round 1–5Steady — pull-ups unbroken, push-ups 10, squats unbroken
  • Round 6–10Pull-ups 3-2, push-ups 5-5, squats unbroken
  • Round 11–15Same structure — do not accelerate yet
  • Round 16–20Hold splits; optional micro-push on final 2 rounds
  • Finish1 mile run — expect legs of concrete; shuffle if needed

Between retests, train the limiter directly: thruster cycling and pull-up volume for Fran, unbroken sets for Cindy, and partitioned engine work for Murph — not endless surprise WODs that never look like the test.

Practical Takeaway

Pick 2–3 benchmarks that match your goals, retest on a clean calendar, and train the limiter (pull-ups, thruster cycling, aerobic base) between tests — don’t live on test day. Warm up specifically for the test movements so the first minute is not your mobility session.

FAQ

Rx or scaled for tracking?

Track the version you can perform with full range and safe mechanics. A clean scaled score beats a broken Rx ego piece.

Can I race Murph every month?

Poor idea for most. It’s a high-recovery-cost test — earn it, then give it space.
