CrossFit Benchmark WODs: Fran, Cindy, Murph, and How to Pace Them

Updated August 2026 · 5 min read

Benchmarks are tests — daily grinding and honest retests conflict. Race Fran every week and you never train the limiter; avoid tests forever and you never know if engine or gymnastics improved. Same standards, comparable scores months apart. Train between tests with EMOMs, the WOD Generator, and Workout Library. Hybrid notes: running + CrossFit.

What Each Tests

  • Fran (21-15-9 thrusters + pull-ups): sprint — break before failure; thruster sets decide most scores
  • Cindy (20 min 5/10/15): steady grind — even rounds beat a hot open
  • Murph (1 mi + 100/200/300 + 1 mi, vest optional): partition thoughtfully; opening mile controlled
  • Helen, Grace, Diane, etc.: same rule — retest under identical standards every 8–12 weeks

Numeric scenario (Murph partitioned 20 rounds of 5/10/15): Open mile at 7:30 when fresh 5K pace is ~7:00/mi often costs the bodyweight block. Controlled open (e.g., 8:15–8:45 for many intermediates), then rounds 1–10 with pull-ups unbroken / push-ups 10 / squats unbroken, rounds 11–20 with pull-ups 3-2 and push-ups 5-5, finish mile as a shuffle if needed — usually beats a sprint open that turns push-ups into singles by round 8.

Expert Note

Log scale, vest, and partitioning with the score. A “faster Murph” with half the pull-ups as jumping reps is a different test. Between retests, train the limiter (thruster cycling, pull-up volume, aerobic base) — not endless novelty WODs that never look like the test.

Decision rule: track the version you can perform with full range and safe mechanics; if projected Fran exceeds ~10 minutes as thruster singles, scale load before using it as a sprint benchmark.

Murph Pacing Snapshot

  • Start1 mile — controlled, not a sprint
  • Rounds 1–5Steady: pull-ups unbroken, push-ups 10, squats unbroken
  • Rounds 6–15Pull-ups 3-2, push-ups 5-5 — do not accelerate yet
  • Rounds 16–20Hold splits; optional micro-push last 2 rounds
  • Finish1 mile — expect heavy legs; shuffle OK

Practical Takeaway

Pick 2–3 benchmarks, retest on a clean calendar, and train the limiter between tests. Warm up specifically so minute one is not your mobility session.

FAQ

Can I race Murph every month?

Poor idea for most. High recovery cost — earn it, then give it space.
