Building Engine for CrossFit: Aerobic Base, Intervals, and Metcon Capacity

Updated August 2026 · 4 min read

Engine is repeatable mixed-modal power — holding cycle rate in minute 14, not surviving one redline. It’s aerobic base + anaerobic repeatability + local muscular endurance.

Set easy boundaries with the Zone 2 Calculator. Build sessions: WOD Generator. Hybrid: Running + CrossFit Balance.

Engine vs Strength Trade-Off

  • Strength block: 3–4 strength · 1–2 hard metcons · 1–2 Zone 2
  • Engine block: 2 strength (exposure) · 2–3 structured conditioning · 2 Zone 2 — accept temporary barbell plateaus
  • In-season/Open: maintain both; novelty/skill > a fifth hard day

Diagnose: great 5-min bike but dying 20-min AMRAPs → base + repeatability. Fine long AMRAPs but thrusters die early → strength/local capacity, not more redline.

Zone 2 and Intervals

True easy (talk test / calculator) — not tempo labeled Zone 2. Minimum 60–90 min/week (2–3 sessions); engine block 120–180. Prefer 30–45 min continuous. Machines OK when running conflicts with squat recovery.

  • EMOM: sustainable power with 15–25 sec rest early — teaches when “comfortable” isn’t
  • Aerobic intervals: e.g., 5×3 min hard / 2 easy; 8×500m — for 12–20 min AMRAP pace
  • Mixed-modal: 90 sec bike + 10 thrusters + 90 rest (moderate loads)
  • Tabata: spice 1–2×/week, not the base

When Engine Is the Limiter

HR spikes early and never settles at light loads; breaks from breathing before local failure; scores crash from 8→20 min domains. Not engine: strong breathing but can’t cycle the bar, skill fails, or grip dies first.

Simple week: Mon upper + short EMOM · Tue Zone 2 40 · Wed lower + skill · Thu aerobic intervals · Fri mixed 12–20 · Sat Zone 2 45–60 · Sun rest. If you take five classes, replace two metcons with Zone 2/technique.

Retest every 4–6 weeks: 2K row/ski, 20-min bike cals, Cindy or a fixed 15–20 min mixed AMRAP.

Practical Takeaway

Protect Zone 2 when it feels “too easy,” use intervals with targets, and keep 2–3 hard metcons — not five floor-staring class days.

FAQ

Is Zone 2 enough?

Necessary, not sufficient. Base + intervals/metcons under barbell/gymnastics interference.

How many hard metcons/week?

Most intermediates: 2–3 hard metcons + 1 structured interval day alongside Zone 2 and strength.
