Building Engine for CrossFit: Aerobic Base, Intervals, and Metcon Capacity

Updated August 2026 · 5 min read

Engine is repeatable mixed-modal power in minute 14 — not surviving one redline. Building it conflicts with chasing weekly barbell PRs: more Zone 2 and structured intervals often mean accepting temporary strength plateaus. Choose the block honestly.

Set easy boundaries with the Zone 2 Calculator. Build sessions: WOD Generator. Hybrid: Running + CrossFit Balance.

Engine vs Strength Trade-Off

  • Strength block: 3–4 strength · 1–2 hard metcons · 1–2 Zone 2
  • Engine block: 2 strength (exposure) · 2–3 structured conditioning · 2 Zone 2
  • In-season/Open: maintain both; novelty/skill > a fifth hard day

Diagnose: great 5-min bike but dying 20-min AMRAPs → base + repeatability. Fine long AMRAPs but thrusters die early → strength/local capacity, not more redline.

Numeric scenario: Athlete takes five classes/week, all RPE 8–9 metcons, zero true Zone 2. Cindy stalls at 14 rounds for two months. Swap two class metcons for 40–45 min Zone 2 (talk test / calculator) + one aerobic interval day (e.g., 5×3 min hard / 2 easy). Retest Cindy in 4–6 weeks — most intermediates see steadier round splits before they need another hero WOD.

Expert Note

True easy is the talk test or calculator ceiling — not tempo labeled Zone 2. Minimum 60–90 min/week (2–3 sessions); engine blocks often 120–180. Machines are fine when running conflicts with squat recovery.

Decision rule: if HR spikes early and never settles at light loads, or scores crash from 8→20 min domains while barbell positions are fine, prioritize Zone 2 + intervals over a fifth floor-staring class.

Intervals That Transfer

  • EMOM: sustainable power with 15–25 sec rest early
  • Aerobic intervals: 5×3 min hard / 2 easy; 8×500m — for 12–20 min AMRAP pace
  • Mixed-modal: 90 sec bike + 10 thrusters + 90 rest (moderate loads)
  • Tabata: spice 1–2×/week, not the base

Simple week: Mon upper + short EMOM · Tue Zone 2 40 · Wed lower + skill · Thu aerobic intervals · Fri mixed 12–20 · Sat Zone 2 45–60 · Sun rest. Retest every 4–6 weeks: 2K row, 20-min bike cals, or a fixed 15–20 min mixed AMRAP.

Practical Takeaway

Protect Zone 2 when it feels “too easy,” use intervals with targets, and keep 2–3 hard metcons — not five.

FAQ

How many hard metcons/week?

Most intermediates: 2–3 hard metcons + 1 structured interval day alongside Zone 2 and strength.
