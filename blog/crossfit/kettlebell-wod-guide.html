Kettlebell WODs: Swings, Goblet Squats, and Metcon Templates

Updated August 2026 · 4 min read

One kettlebell covers swings, goblet squats, cleans, presses, and carries. Grip and hinge stamina usually fail before “cardio” does — load and set schemes matter. Pair with AMRAPs and EMOMs, or build KB metcons in the WOD Generator.

Key Points

  • Russian swings (chest height) vs American (overhead) — match the workout standard
  • Hinge from the hips; don’t squat the swing or yank with the low back
  • Goblet squats: elbows inside knees, depth you own under fatigue
  • When form breaks, drop load or switch to unbroken smaller sets — scaling
  • Similar toolbox: dumbbell WODs

Swings and Goblet Squats

Swings: snap the hips, float the bell, guide with the arms. Soft lockout and rounded back are stop signs. For American swings, only go overhead if shoulders and midline stay stacked — otherwise Russian swings keep power without risking the press-out.

Goblet squats: hold the horns or body of the bell at the chest, sit between the heels, drive up without collapsing the torso. High-rep goblets punish breathing — plan breaks like thrusters.

  • Light / high-rep: 12–16 kg (women) / 16–20 kg (men) as a common starting band — individualize
  • Moderate metcon: 16–20 / 20–24 kg
  • Heavy power: 20–24 / 24–32 kg for lower reps

Metcon Templates

AMRAP 10 — Swing Engine

  • Round15 KB Swings · 10 Goblet Squats · 8 Push-ups

Target: 5–7 rounds · break swings 8-7 before form fades

EMOM 12 — Grip + Legs

  • Odd12–15 Russian swings
  • Even8–10 goblet squats · leave ≥12 sec rest

Drop reps if rest disappears — density without junk reps

For Time — Simple Triplet

  • 21-15-9Swings · Goblet Squats · Burpees (or cal bike)

Open at a pace you can hold through 15s; see For Time pacing

Two KB metcons per week is plenty beside lifting and running. Avoid heavy swing volume the day before a long run or max deadlift.

Practical Takeaway

Own the hinge, pick a load that survives the last round, and use small planned sets. Power and positions beat chasing a heavier bell. More ideas in the Workout Library.

FAQ

Russian or American swings in WODs?

Follow the posted standard. If unspecified and overhead position is limited, Russian swings keep hip power without forcing a risky lockout.

Only one KB available?

Favor swings, goblets, single-arm cleans/presses, and carries. Raise density with EMOMs or longer AMRAPs instead of forcing barbell-equivalent loads.
