CrossFit Weekly Programming: How to Structure Strength, Metcons, and Rest

Updated August 2026 · 5 min read

Showing up six days a week and recovering from six hard days conflict. Random hard WODs work briefly, then flatten you. Programming is fatigue accounting: strength, metcons, skills, and rest share one recovery budget.

Hybrid notes: Running and CrossFit Balance. Build sessions with the WOD Generator and Workout Library.

Weekly Budget

Spend three currencies: neural/strength, metabolic, and local tissue (grip, shoulders, lumbar, Achilles). Different-looking hard days that spend the same currency still stack.

For 4–5 days/week non-competitors: ~70–80% easy/moderate · ~15–25% hard · infrequent true tests. Cap Hard days at 2–3 total (including hard runs). If every class is RPE 9, distribution is inverted.

Numeric scenario: Athlete trains Mon–Fri classes, labels all five “moderate,” but each ends RPE 8–9. Sleep drops; Friday Fran is 90 sec slower than last month. Relabel: Mon heavy lower + ≤10 min metcon (Hard) · Wed OL + skill + 12–15 moderate · Fri harder For Time (Hard) · Tue/Thu technique or Zone 2. Two Hard days beat five redlines for most intermediates over 8–12 weeks.

Expert Note

After a benchmark PR, schedule 24–48 hours easy (technique, Zone 2) — not another chipper. Deload every 4–6 weeks with ~40–50% volume/intensity.

Decision rule: when sleep, mood, or scores slide, cut Hard days before cutting frequency. Five days with two hard sessions can work; five RPE 8–9 metcons cannot.

Hard-Day Spacing & Samples

  • Max lower strength vs high-volume squat/lunge metcons: 48+ hr
  • Heavy Olympic lifting vs Fran/Isabel-style cycling: ≥1 day
  • Dense gymnastics: 2–3×/week max, 48 hr if joints sensitive

3 days: Mon lower + short metcon · Wed upper/OL + skill + moderate · Fri For Time/longer AMRAP.

4 days (sweet spot): Mon heavy squat/DL + ≤10 min · Wed OL + skill + 12–15 · Fri upper + harder For Time · Sat longer engine. Hard = Mon/Fri/Sat.

5 days: add Tue gymnastics + aerobic Zone-3 metcon (row/bike bias). If Wed becomes a hero WOD, drop to 4.

6 days: only with honest easy days + Sunday rest. Same-day heavy (≥80%, ≤6 reps) → metcon ≤10–12 min at ≤45% cycling loads.

Hybrids: long run after rest or upper-biased CF; quality run 48 hr from heavy lower. Cap Hard at 2–3 across both sports.

Practical Takeaway

Pick the lowest frequency you can finish for 8–12 weeks. Label days Easy/Moderate/Hard before the week starts.

FAQ

Strength and metcon same day?

Default skill → strength → metcon. Separate days when both need to be hard. After heavy lower, keep the metcon short and lighter.
