Best EMOM Workouts for Every Level

Updated August 2026 · 4 min read

EMOM (Every Minute On the Minute): start the prescribed work at :00, rest with whatever time remains. Unlike AMRAP, rest is forced by the clock. Need a quick session? Use the WOD Generator (EMOM format).

Key Points

  • Minute 1 should feel almost easy; later minutes get tight
  • Target ~15–25 sec rest early; if rest <10 sec by mid-session, cut reps
  • Scale reps, not “no rest.” Alternate upper/lower to manage local fatigue
  • Wrist HR lags on mixed work — track rest time + RPE; use the Calories Calculator only as a rough guide
  • 1–2 hard EMOMs/week is enough for most hybrid athletes

Templates

EMOM 10 — Bodyweight Basics

  • Min 110 Air Squats
  • Min 28 Push-ups
  • Min 312 Sit-ups
  • Min 48 Lunges (each leg)
  • Min 510 Mountain Climbers (each leg)

Repeat for 2 rounds (10 min total)

EMOM 12 — Cardio + Core

  • Min 115 Single Unders
  • Min 210 Air Squats
  • Min 330 sec Plank Hold
  • Min 48 Burpees

Repeat for 3 rounds (12 min total)

EMOM 20 — The Engine Builder

  • Min 112 KB Swings (24/16 kg)
  • Min 210 Box Jumps (24/20")
  • Min 38 DB Push Press (each arm)
  • Min 415/12 Cal Row
  • Min 510 Burpees

Repeat for 4 rounds (20 min total)

EMOM 24 — Barbell Complex Lite

  • Min 15 Power Cleans (95/65 lb)
  • Min 28 Pull-ups
  • Min 310 Wall Balls (20/14 lb)
  • Min 412/10 Cal Assault Bike
  • Min 56 Front Squats (95/65 lb)
  • Min 620 Double Unders

Repeat for 4 rounds (24 min total)

Practical Takeaway

Log slowest minute, average rest, and RPE. Progress reps or add a round only after two stable sessions. Advanced loaded EMOMs belong 48+ hours after heavy squats if you also run.

FAQ

EMOM vs AMRAP — which builds engine better?

EMOM teaches sustainable output with fixed work; AMRAP rewards aggressive packing of reps. Use both.

How do I know reps are too high?

If minute 1 is a gasp, or rest falls under ~10 seconds by the middle, cut reps 20–30% and restart with sustainable mechanics.
