Best EMOM Workouts for Every Level

Updated August 2026 · 5 min read

EMOM forces work at :00 and rests with whatever remains — density and recovery conflict every minute. Pack too many reps and rest vanishes by mid-session; pack too few and the clock never teaches sustainable power. Unlike AMRAP, the rest is external. Build sessions in the WOD Generator (EMOM format).

Rest Rules

  • Minute 1 should feel almost easy; later minutes get tight
  • Target ~15–25 sec rest early; if rest <10 sec by mid-session, cut reps
  • Scale reps, not “no rest.” Alternate upper/lower to manage local fatigue
  • Wrist HR lags — track rest + RPE; the Calories Calculator is a rough daily aid only

Numeric scenario: EMOM 12 — 10 thrusters (75/55). Fresh set of 10 takes 22 sec → 38 sec rest. By minute 7, if the set takes 48 sec (12 sec rest), cut to 7–8 reps next session. Holding 15–20 sec rest across all 12 minutes beats finishing “Rx” with zero rest for the last four minutes.

Expert Note

EMOM “missed minutes” usually mean the prescription was a For Time piece in disguise. Protect the rest — that is the training signal. Log slowest minute and average rest, not only whether you finished.

Decision rule: if rest falls under ~10 seconds by the middle third, cut reps 20–30% (or load) and restart; do not delete rest to keep the whiteboard Rx.

Templates

EMOM 10 — Bodyweight Basics

  • Min 110 Air Squats
  • Min 28 Push-ups
  • Min 312 Sit-ups
  • Min 48 Lunges (each leg)
  • Min 510 Mountain Climbers (each leg)

Repeat 2× (10 min) · keep ≥15 sec rest early

EMOM 12 — Cardio + Core

  • Min 115 Single Unders
  • Min 210 Air Squats
  • Min 330 sec Plank
  • Min 48 Burpees

Repeat 3× (12 min)

EMOM 20 — Engine Builder

  • Min 112 KB Swings (24/16 kg)
  • Min 210 Box Jumps (24/20")
  • Min 38 DB Push Press (each)
  • Min 415/12 Cal Row
  • Min 510 Burpees

Repeat 4× (20 min) · cut row cals if rest <10 sec

EMOM 24 — Barbell Complex Lite

  • Min 15 Power Cleans (95/65 lb)
  • Min 28 Pull-ups
  • Min 310 Wall Balls (20/14 lb)
  • Min 412/10 Cal Bike
  • Min 56 Front Squats (95/65 lb)
  • Min 620 Double Unders

Repeat 4× (24 min) · 48+ hr after heavy squats if you also run

Practical Takeaway

1–2 hard EMOMs/week is enough for most hybrids. Progress reps or add a round only after two sessions with stable rest.

FAQ

How do I know reps are too high?

Minute 1 is a gasp, or rest falls under ~10 seconds by the middle — cut 20–30% and rebuild.
