AMRAP Workouts: Pacing, Programming, and Templates for Every Level

Updated August 2026 · 5 min read

AMRAP rewards packing work into a fixed clock — but round-1 speed and total score often conflict. Open too hot and you donate rounds later; open too soft and you leave reps on the floor. You own the rests; pacing is the skill. Compare formats with EMOM; build sessions in the WOD Generator and Workout Library.

Pacing Before the Clock

  • EMOM fixes work/rest; For Time races a fixed task; AMRAP maximizes work in a window
  • Plan a sustainable round time in minute 1; negative-split when possible
  • Cap hard AMRAPs near long runs — running + CrossFit balance
  • Wearable calories on mixed AMRAP are noisy — use the Calories Calculator as a planning aid only

Numeric scenario: AMRAP 12 of 5 pull-ups / 10 push-ups / 15 air squats (30 reps/round). Target 9 rounds → 720 ÷ 9 = 80 sec/round. Round 1 at 65 sec (RPE ~8) usually drifts to 95–100 sec by minute 10 and finishes ~7–8 rounds. Round 1 at 75–78 sec (RPE ~6–7) more often holds 8–9 rounds with ≤8% split drift.

Expert Note

Score the last two minutes the same way you score the first two. If push-up range or pull-up chin-over-bar standards collapse, the “extra” round is not comparable next month — log the scale and standards with the score.

Decision rule: if round 1 takes >15% longer than your planned split, scale reps or skill before round 2 — don’t “hope” it settles.

Templates

AMRAP 8 — Foundation

  • Round8 Air Squats · 6 Push-ups (knee/box OK) · 10 Sit-ups

Target: 8+ rounds · 24 reps/round · incline push-ups if you drop below 6 rounds

AMRAP 10 — Cardio Starter

  • Round10 Single Unders · 8 Walking Lunges · 6 Ring Rows

Target: 7–9 rounds · hybrid-friendly on easy-run days

AMRAP 12 — Classic Triplet

  • Round5 Pull-ups · 10 Push-ups · 15 Air Squats

Target: 8–10 rounds (240–300 reps) · retest monthly under identical standards

AMRAP 16 — Engine + Strength

  • Round12/10 Cal Row · 8 KB Swings (24/16 kg) · 6 Box Jumps (24/20")

Target: 5–7 rounds · keep row splits within ~5% across rounds

Practical Takeaway

Progress by steadier round splits and unbroken sets that survive the last two minutes — not only total reps. Place hard AMRAPs away from long runs and heavy lower-body days when you can.

FAQ

How hard should minute one feel?

About 6–7/10. If you’re redlining immediately, you won’t hold the planned split.
