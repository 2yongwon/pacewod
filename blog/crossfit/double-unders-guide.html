Double Unders for CrossFit: Progressions, Fatigue Management, and WOD Strategy

Updated August 2026 · 4 min read

Double unders are a calf, shoulder, and composure test under fatigue — not just a coordination trick. Fresh 50s often fragment after thrusters without a break plan.

Format context: AMRAP, For Time. Scale via scaling guidance. Practice: WOD Generator.

Setup and Progression

Stand on midpoint; handles ≈ armpit to lower rib. Speed cable once you clear 20+. Log max unbroken and time for 50 monthly (solid intermediate: 40–60 unbroken · 50 in 0:30–0:45).

  1. Singles: 100 quiet; 2 min continuous without a trip
  2. Single-single-double: 20 doubles via pattern in <3 min work
  3. Sets of 3–5: exit at 3×10 unbroken
  4. Capacity: 25→40→50 unbroken; add fatigue only after fresh 50 twice in a week

Cue wrist speed (“spin first, then jump”) before “jump higher.”

Trip Recovery and Set Math

Good recovery: stop bounce → open rope → 2 breaths → restart at 80% for 3–5 reps. Target 3–5 sec from trip to first success (vs 8–15 sec freeze). Three trips at 4 sec vs 12 sec = ~24 sec saved on 120 DUs.

Let U = reliable unbroken fresh. Mid-WOD ≈50–70% of U; late AMRAP ≈40–60%.

  • Set ≤50% U → unbroken
  • 50–80% U → one quick break (e.g., 40-20 for 60)
  • >80% U or already gassed → smaller sets from the start

100 DUs: planned 25×4 with 5-sec rests often beats fail-at-55 then mess. Annie 50s: plan 30-20 unless tested.

Calf Management and Scaling

Increase weekly DU volume ≤20–25%. Max one hard DU metcon/day; avoid DU-dense days back-to-back while building. Hybrid runners: 24–36 hr from quality track/long runs. Scale: 2–3× singles matching time domain; avoid 5× singles that turn a 90-sec station into 4 minutes.

Samples: 5 rounds 15 thrusters + 40 DUs; AMRAP 10: 10 swings + 20 DUs (8–10 rounds).

Practical Takeaway

Progress with exit criteria, plan breaks at 50–70% of fresh max inside metcons, and train 3–5 second trip resets — composure beats unbroken pride.

FAQ

Why fresh doubles but not in WODs?

Heart rate, grip, and rushed resets. Practice after small thruster/burpee/row doses, and lower unbroken ambitions to 50–70% of fresh max.

Singles or scale reps?

Use 2–3× singles when you can still match the time domain. Don’t grind ultra-high single volumes that miss the metabolic point.
