Double Unders for CrossFit: Progressions, Fatigue Management, and WOD Strategy

Updated August 2026 · 5 min read

Wanting unbroken pride and needing composure after thrusters often conflict. Fresh 50s fragment in metcons without a break plan. Context: AMRAP, For Time, scaling, WOD Generator.

Progression With Exit Criteria

  1. Singles: 100 quiet; 2 min continuous without a trip
  2. Single-single-double pattern until 20 doubles in <3 min work
  3. Sets of 3–5 → exit at 3×10 unbroken
  4. Build 25→40→50 unbroken; add fatigue only after fresh 50 twice in a week

Numeric scenario: Reliable unbroken fresh (U) = 60. Mid-WOD sets ≈50–70% of U (30–40). For 100 DUs, planned 25×4 with 5-sec rests often beats fail-at-55 then chaos. Trip reset target: 3–5 sec to first success (vs 8–15 sec freeze) — three trips at 4 sec vs 12 sec ≈24 sec saved.

Expert Note

Cue wrist speed before “jump higher.” Most trip spirals are panic bouncing, not missing a centimeter of height.

Decision rule: set ≤50% U → unbroken; 50–80% U → one quick break; >80% U or already gassed → smaller sets from the start.

Calf Management

Increase weekly DU volume ≤20–25%. Max one hard DU metcon/day while building. Hybrid runners: 24–36 hr from quality track/long runs. Scale with 2–3× singles matching time domain — not 5× singles that miss the stimulus.

Practice Structure That Transfers

Fresh practice builds the skill; lightly fatigued practice transfers to WODs — e.g., 10 thrusters then DU sets. Film a few attempts monthly to check posture and elbow position. Rope length: stand on the midpoint; handles near armpit to lower rib as a starting fit. Log max unbroken and time for 50 as monthly markers. If calves stay sore into the next quality run, you did too much too soon — cut metcon DU density and keep short technical doses.

When trips cluster, stop and reset your hands rather than speeding up. A calm 40 with one planned break beats a chaotic 55 that costs 20 seconds of rope fighting. Keep spare cables if you train outdoors on abrasive surfaces.

Practical Takeaway

Plan breaks at 50–70% of fresh max inside metcons, and train fast trip resets — composure beats unbroken pride.

FAQ

Why fresh doubles but not in WODs?

Heart rate, grip, and rushed resets. Practice after small thruster/burpee doses, and lower unbroken ambitions mid-WOD.
