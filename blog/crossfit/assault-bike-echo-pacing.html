Assault Bike and Echo Bike Pacing: Calories, RPM, and Metcon Strategy

Updated August 2026 · 4 min read

Fan bikes punish ego. Calories climb when you stay smooth; they stall when you sprint then die. Assault/Air and Echo feel different, but the pacing skill is the same: pick a sustainable RPM window and hold round splits. Build pieces in the WOD Generator; compare formats in the AMRAP guide.

Key Points

  • Drive with legs first; arms finish — not the reverse
  • RPM is your “pace”: note what cal/min you hold at 55–65 vs 70–80 RPM
  • Echo often feels heavier at the same RPM; don’t copy a friend’s Assault numbers
  • Wearable calories ≠ bike calories — use the console for the workout, and the Calories Calculator only as a rough daily aid
  • Negative-split cal buys when possible (see For Time)

Calories and RPM Anchors

Test a 2–3 minute steady effort: record average RPM and cal/min. That becomes your metcon ceiling for 8–15 minute pieces. Example anchors (highly individual):

  • Easy recovery spin: conversational, ~40–55 RPM
  • Sustainable metcon: ~55–70 RPM with unbroken breathing
  • Short sprint buys: 15–30 sec surge, then return to base RPM

If cal/min drops >15% mid-WOD, you opened too hot — drop 5 RPM next round.

Metcon Strategy

AMRAP / Chipper Bike Buys

  • RuleFirst buy at 80–85% of tested cal/min; last buy at 95–100%

Leave the bike before legs lock so transitions to barbell/gymnastics stay clean

Couplets with Squats or Burpees

  • RuleCap bike at a pace you can leave and still unbroken the next movement

Example: 12/10 cal + 10 thrusters — bike should not force thruster singles by round 3

Sample Pieces

  • EMOM 12: odd 12/10 cal · even 8–10 air squats or KB swings · ≥10 sec rest
  • AMRAP 10: 10/8 cal + 8 push-ups + 12 lunges · hold cal time within 5 sec each round
  • For Time: 30-20-10 cal + burpees · open cal ~10% slower than max 2-min test

More engine ideas: engine conditioning, rowing pacing.

Practical Takeaway

Know your steady RPM → cal/min map, open slightly conservative, and protect the movements after the bike. Consistency beats a heroic first buy.

FAQ

Assault vs Echo — which is harder?

Depends on machine and athlete. Echo often demands more at the same RPM. Pace by your console data on the bike you’ll use in the workout.

Should I watch watts or calories?

For CrossFit metcons, calories (or cals/min) match the scorecard. Use watts/RPM as the internal governor that keeps cal splits honest.
