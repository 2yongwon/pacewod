CrossFit Recovery Between Workouts: Sleep, Soft Tissue, and When to Deload

Updated August 2026 · 4 min read

CrossFit recovers on a schedule whether you respect it. Heavy strength, eccentric metcons, and high-skill gymnastics leave different residue — plan the next day by system stressed, not vibes.

Hybrid: Running + CrossFit Balance. Sleep mechanisms: Sleep and Running Performance. Easier sessions: WOD Generator.

24–48 Hour Rules

Often OK within 24 hr: technique/low eccentric; short metcons ~8–10 min moderate; opposite-body-region strength; Zone 2 if impact allows.

Prefer ~48 hr before repeating: heavy squat/DL (>~80% multi-set); high-volume box jumps/burpees/run metcons; hero chippers/long AMRAPs; high-volume kipping that irritates shoulders/midline; dense partner burpee-run work.

Train tomorrow — just not the same tissue the same way. Soreness peaks 24–48 hr; feeling “fine” at 12 hr is early, not cleared.

Sleep, Soft Tissue, Active Recovery

  • Sleep: 7.5–9 hours; consistent timing; protect the night after the hardest session
  • Soft tissue: 5–10 minutes on loaded tissues + light movement through range — not a 45-minute substitute for sleep/food
  • Active recovery: true Zone 2 (calculator), walk, easy row/bike 20–40 min — not a casual Fran

RHR, HRV, and Deloads

RHR up ~5–8+ bpm above baseline for several days (with poor sleep/heavy legs) → cut intensity. One noisy morning isn’t a crisis. Use HRV as multi-day trend + subjective readiness — not a daily veto. Trends over 7–14 days beat green/red theater.

Deload every 3–6 hard weeks, or after Open/throwdown: cut metcon/strength volume ~40–60%, remove maxes/redlines, keep Zone 2 and short fresh skill. A hero WOD “for fun” voids the deload.

Sample Sequencing

Day 1 heavy lower + short metcon · Day 2 Zone 2 + upper skill · Day 3 moderate mixed · Day 4 rest/walk · Day 5 OL + gymnastics · Day 6 longer engine or partner · Day 7 rest. After burpee-run day, don’t long-run the next morning.

Practical Takeaway

Match the next session to a different system, guard sleep like a training day, and deload before injury forces it — especially when RHR trends up and performance stalls.

FAQ

CrossFit two days in a row?

Yes if stressors differ (heavy lower → easy upper/Zone 2). Two redline leg-dense days back-to-back is junk fatigue.

How often to deload?

Most intermediates: lighter week every 4–6 hard weeks, or right after a competition block. Keep at least one true easy day inside hard weeks.
