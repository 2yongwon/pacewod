Tabata Workouts for CrossFit: 20/10 Intervals, Movement Selection, and Templates

Updated August 2026 · 5 min read

Wanting round-1 hero reps and needing a score that survives round 8 often conflict. Classic Tabata is 20 sec work / 10 sec rest × 8 (4 minutes) — a sharp spike, not a full engine day. Build with the WOD Generator; related: EMOM, running balance.

Score the Lowest Round

  • Choose movements you can repeat with safe form when tired
  • Many scores use lowest-round reps — pace so round 8 doesn’t collapse
  • 1–2 hard Tabata exposures/week is enough for most
  • Don’t stack after heavy squats + run intervals the same day

Numeric scenario: Air-squat Tabata targeting 16/round. Opening at 22, then 18, 14, 11… yields a weak lowest-round score. Opening at 15–16 and holding ±2 through round 8 usually beats the fade. If round 3 is already −4 vs round 1, cut effort immediately.

Expert Note

Mixed 20/10 circuits are fine cousins of the classic single-movement protocol. What matters is repeatable positions under fatigue — not claiming you “did Tabata science” with thrusters after a max snatch.

Decision rule: if form breaks (depth, lockout, landing) before round 4, scale the movement or drop target reps — don’t negotiate standards mid-set.

Templates

Classic Air Squat

  • Format20 sec max air squats / 10 sec rest × 8
  • Target14–18/round; lowest within 4 of highest

Assault Bike Calories

  • Format20 sec max cal / 10 sec rest × 8
  • Target~12–16 cal/round (machine-dependent)

Push + Squat Alternating

  • Rounds 1–420 sec push-ups / 10 sec rest
  • Rounds 5–820 sec air squats / 10 sec rest

Double Unders

  • Format20 sec max DUs / 10 sec rest × 8
  • ScaleSingles ×2 or penguin steps

Pairing With Running Weeks

Treat hard Tabata as a quality dose. If you already have intervals or a long run, keep Tabata as a short finisher on a lighter day — or skip it. Hybrid athletes: 1 true redline Tabata exposure midweek is plenty beside a weekend long run. Score and log movement standards so retests mean something. Progress by raising the lowest round, then optionally adding a second 4-minute block with full recovery — not by grinding junk sets after form collapses. Practical Takeaway

Progress by raising the lowest-round count — not by adding junk volume after you’re cooked.

FAQ

Best movements for Tabata?

Simple, repeatable patterns: squats, bike/rower calories, controlled push-ups, double-unders. Avoid max-skill barbell cycling when tired.
